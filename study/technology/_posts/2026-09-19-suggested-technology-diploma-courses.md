# 🎓 Technology Diploma Shelf — Courses Worth Bookmarking

## 📚 A Shelf for Later

One of the useful things about learning online is that we don't have to learn everything at once.

Sometimes the sensible thing to do is simply **find a good course, bookmark it, and know where it is when we're ready for it.**

That is what this page is about.

PlebWare is building a growing technology learning path, and there are more courses available than any one person could realistically complete at the same time.

So instead of saying:

> "You must do this course now."

we are building a **Technology Diploma Shelf**.

These are courses worth knowing about and potentially adding to your future learning list.

## 🧭 So Where Should You Start?

There is no requirement to tackle all of these courses.

In fact, trying to do everything simultaneously is probably a good way to turn learning into another job.

A sensible technology learning path could look something like this:

**Level 1 — Understand AI**

Start with an introductory AI course and learn the basic vocabulary.

**Level 2 — Understand Programming**

Learn Python fundamentals and basic programming concepts.

**Level 3 — Understand Applied AI**

Explore Generative AI and how AI is being used in practical situations.

**Level 4 — Understand Data**

Learn how Python can be used with data.

**Level 5 — Understand Machine Learning**

Move into machine learning concepts and algorithms.

**Level 6 — Go Deeper**

Explore more specialised subjects such as neural networks, TensorFlow, APIs, local AI and AI application development.

The important thing is that the learner controls the pace.

## 📌 Bookmark First. Study Later.

There is another important lesson here.

**A bookmark is not a commitment.**

You don't have to enrol in six courses because you found six interesting courses.

You don't have to finish a course because you started it.

And you don't have to become a programmer simply because you are interested in artificial intelligence.

Learning works better when curiosity leads the way.

The PlebWare approach is simple:

> **Find it. Bookmark it. Learn it when you're ready.**

## ⚠️ A Word About the Word "Diploma"

There is an important distinction to make.

The courses listed here are **online diploma courses offered by Alison**.

That does not automatically make them equivalent to a South African university qualification, TVET qualification or an NQF-registered diploma.

The purpose of this PlebWare list is to identify **accessible learning opportunities**, not to claim that an online course carries the same status as a formal tertiary qualification.

Always check the recognition requirements that apply to your particular career, employer or profession.
