# 🎨 Comics — The Art of Visual Storytelling.

Sequential art, graphic storytelling, and comic creation.

I most certainly do not consider myself a serious comic creator, and I am definitely not in the same league as **Charles M. Schulz**, creator of *Peanuts*, featuring Charlie Brown, or **Antonio Prohías**, the Cuban-born cartoonist who created *Spy vs. Spy* for *MAD Magazine*.

However, I love cartoons and their remarkable ability to say things that words alone sometimes cannot.

I use Artificial Intelligence (AI) to help create my own cartoons. Although these are my creative projects, there are several important considerations worth discussing. AI opens exciting possibilities for independent creators, but it also presents challenges involving artistic direction, technical consistency, copyright, production methods, audience perceptions, and ethical responsibility.

Let's take a closer look! 🚀

<!-- PLEBVOX:END -->

<!-- PLEBVOX:START -->

## 🎭 1. Intent and Curation.

Generative AI tools do not possess human intentionality, lived experience, or personal philosophy in the way people do.

The human creator using AI acts as the storyteller, art director, prompt writer, and editor. The creator decides what the cartoon should communicate, which images are suitable, how the panels should be arranged, and whether the finished work achieves its intended purpose.

The validity of a creative work does not depend solely on the tool used to produce its images. Storytelling, pacing, humour, clarity, and emotional resonance all contribute to its success.

A beautiful picture is not necessarily a good cartoon. The expression on a character's face, the timing of a punchline, or the contrast between two panels can communicate an idea more effectively than a lengthy explanation.

🔑 **The technology helps produce the artwork, but the creator directs the story and takes responsibility for the finished result.**

## 🎨 2. Consistency Challenges.

Traditional comic art relies heavily on visual continuity. Characters must remain recognisable from one panel to the next, including their facial features, body proportions, clothing, hairstyles, and other defining characteristics.

AI image generation can make this difficult.

Imagine a character appearing in one panel with a particular face and outfit, only to acquire a different nose, a new jacket, and an entirely different hairstyle in the next. Unless the change is intentional, the reader may struggle to recognise the character.

Many image-generation workflows create each image separately. Even when the same text prompt is reused, the results can vary. Reusing a random seed does not, by itself, guarantee consistent characters across different scenes.

### 🧩 How Can We Improve Consistency?

Creators can combine traditional digital-art techniques with AI-assisted controls to improve continuity.

- 🧍 **Character reference sheets:** Establish the character's appearance from different angles, including facial expressions, clothing, and proportions.
- 🖼️ **Reference images:** Use approved images to guide subsequent generations when the chosen tool supports this feature.
- 📝 **Consistent descriptions:** Reuse carefully developed descriptions of characters, clothing, and settings.
- 🎨 **Style references:** Maintain consistent line work, colour palettes, shading, and visual presentation.
- 🧱 **Reusable assets:** Keep recurring characters, objects, and backgrounds available for future panels.
- ✂️ **Manual editing:** Correct inconsistencies using digital-art software rather than accepting every generated image as finished work.
- 🎛️ **Image guidance tools:** Where available, use pose controls, composition guidance, masks, or reference-conditioning features to influence the generated result.

Maintaining character consistency across dozens or hundreds of panels remains one of the biggest challenges in AI-assisted visual storytelling. It requires planning, patience, and a repeatable production workflow.

## ⚖️ 3. Copyright Eligibility.

Copyright law is an important consideration for anyone creating comics with AI.

The legal position varies between countries, and the rules continue to develop. In the United States, the U.S. Copyright Office has explained that material generated entirely by AI does not automatically qualify for copyright protection simply because a person supplied a prompt.

However, human-authored elements may qualify for protection when the applicable legal requirements are met. These can include original dialogue, scripts, storytelling, creative selection and arrangement of material, panel composition, and sufficiently original human-created artwork.

For example, a comic might combine AI-generated illustrations with a human-written script, carefully planned panels, original dialogue, and a distinctive narrative. The copyright status of its individual elements may differ from that of the complete work.

It is therefore important not to assume that every AI-generated image is automatically protected, or that every work containing AI-generated material is automatically unprotected.

🌍 Copyright rules differ internationally, including between South Africa and the United States. Anyone intending to publish or commercially exploit AI-assisted comics should check the applicable law and the terms of the tools they use.

🔑 Keeping original scripts, drafts, reference material, and records of human editing can help document the creative process, although these records do not guarantee copyright protection.

## 🛠️ 4. Production Tooling.

AI tools are increasingly being explored as part of digital comic-production workflows. They can assist with brainstorming, concept development, preliminary compositions, background generation, visual references, and repetitive tasks.

For independent creators, these capabilities can reduce some of the practical barriers to experimentation.

AI does not have to replace traditional drawing and editing. It can work alongside established digital-art applications.

Examples include:

- 🎨 **Krita:** Digital painting, drawing, and image editing.
- ✒️ **Clip Studio Paint:** Illustration, comic panels, and lettering workflows.
- 🖌️ **Adobe Photoshop:** Image manipulation, compositing, retouching, and refinement.
- 🤖 **AI image-generation tools:** Concept exploration, image creation, and visual experimentation, depending on their capabilities.

The best combination depends on the creator's budget, operating system, experience, and preferred workflow.

### 🚀 A Practical Production Workflow.

An AI-assisted comic might follow these steps:

1. 💡 Develop the idea and decide what the story should communicate.
2. ✍️ Write the script, including dialogue, narration, and punchlines.
3. 🧍 Establish character designs and reference sheets.
4. 📋 Plan the panels and the sequence of events.
5. 🤖 Generate suitable visual material using AI.
6. 🔍 Check character consistency and continuity.
7. ✂️ Edit the images and correct mistakes.
8. 💬 Add speech bubbles, captions, and lettering.
9. ✅ Review the complete comic for readability, spelling, pacing, and visual clarity.

The workflow can vary from project to project. Some creators begin with a script, while others experiment with images before developing the story.

The objective is to use the available tools intelligently while retaining creative control.

## 👀 5. Reader Perception.

Audience opinions about AI-generated artwork remain divided.

Some readers appreciate the speed, experimentation, and imaginative possibilities of AI-assisted creativity. Others prefer traditionally illustrated comics because they value the artist's individual technique, craftsmanship, and personal expression.

Some readers also want creators to disclose when AI has contributed substantially to the finished work.

These perspectives deserve consideration. The production method is only one part of the discussion; the quality of the storytelling matters too.

Does the cartoon make someone laugh? Does it expose hypocrisy? Does it challenge an assumption or help explain a complicated subject?

A technically impressive image can still fail if the joke is confusing or the narrative is weak. Conversely, a simple illustration can be remarkably effective when the idea and timing are right.

😂 **Never underestimate the power of a well-timed visual punchline!**

## 🧭 6. Training Data Ethics.

One of the major debates surrounding generative AI concerns the artwork and other material used to train AI models.

Many artists object to their work being used for training without their consent or compensation. Others are concerned about imitation of distinctive artistic styles, competition for creative work, attribution, and the potential impact on artists' livelihoods.

These concerns have contributed to legal disputes and ongoing discussions about copyright, licensing, fair use, and responsible AI development.

The legal position depends on the jurisdiction and circumstances. It would be misleading to claim that every use of copyrighted material for AI training is either lawful or unlawful in every situation.

There are ethical questions to consider as well as legal ones.

### 🤝 Responsible Creative Practices.

As creators, we can make informed choices about the tools we use.

- 📚 Read the terms and conditions of AI services.
- 🔍 Check the licensing and provenance of reference material where possible.
- ✍️ Develop original characters, scripts, and stories.
- 🛑 Do not present another artist's existing work as your own.
- 🗣️ Be transparent about AI involvement when appropriate or required.
- ⚖️ Respect applicable intellectual-property laws and licensing conditions.

AI-assisted creativity offers opportunities, but responsible use requires more than knowing how to generate an image.

🌱 **Innovation and respect for human creativity should go hand in hand.**

## 🧠 7. The Technical Challenge of Character Consistency.

Maintaining character consistency across a complete comic deserves particular attention because it affects almost every part of visual storytelling.

Standard image-generation workflows may interpret the same character differently in separate generations. Facial features, clothing, body proportions, lighting, and even the overall art style can shift.

Text prompts alone often cannot guarantee reliable continuity across a long narrative.

Creators can address this by combining traditional digital-art pipelines with more advanced AI control techniques.

Depending on the available software, these approaches may include:

- 🖼️ Reference-image conditioning to guide character appearance.
- 🧍 Character sheets showing approved facial features, clothing, and proportions.
- 🎛️ Pose and composition controls to guide the placement of figures and objects.
- 🧩 Layered editing to separate characters from backgrounds.
- ✂️ Manual corrections to repair details that AI produces inconsistently.
- 📖 Continuity notes documenting characters, settings, props, and important story events.

Some workflows use specialised models, adapters, or other reference-guidance systems. Their effectiveness depends on the software and the task, and no single method guarantees perfect results.

Traditional digital-art applications such as Krita, Clip Studio Paint, and Photoshop can complement these techniques by allowing creators to edit, composite, and refine the generated material.

The essential lesson is that creating a complete comic requires more than generating a collection of attractive images.

Every panel must contribute to a coherent narrative, and recurring characters must remain recognisable while expressing different emotions and performing different actions.

🎯 **The goal is to build a repeatable workflow that supports consistent, readable, and engaging visual storytelling.**

## ✍️ My Personal Perspective.

I make no grand claims about being a professional comic artist. I simply love cartoons, enjoy experimenting with AI, and appreciate the many ways pictures can communicate ideas.

For me, this is part of the creative adventure.

A cartoon can entertain, educate, challenge authority, expose hypocrisy, or make a complicated subject easier to understand. Sometimes a few panels can communicate an idea that would require several paragraphs of explanation.

AI gives independent creators another way to explore their imagination, but the technology does not eliminate the need for planning, editing, judgement, or storytelling.

Sometimes a generated image will be exactly what I wanted. Sometimes it will be completely wrong. Occasionally, it will produce something unexpected that inspires an entirely new idea.

That is part of the process: experiment, evaluate, improve, and keep learning.

I may never create another *Peanuts* or *Spy vs. Spy*, but I can still enjoy developing my own cartoons and finding my own creative voice.

## 🌟 Final Thoughts.

The technology is a tool. The imagination gives the work direction, the storytelling gives it meaning, and the creator remains responsible for the choices that shape the finished result.

AI-assisted cartooning presents exciting opportunities alongside technical, ethical, and legal challenges. By combining thoughtful storytelling, suitable tools, careful editing, and respect for other creators, independent artists can explore those opportunities responsibly.

So, let us keep experimenting, keep learning, and keep discovering new ways to turn ideas into pictures.

And who knows? Perhaps the next cartoon will make somebody laugh, think, or see the world a little differently.

That, to me, is reason enough to keep creating! 🎨
