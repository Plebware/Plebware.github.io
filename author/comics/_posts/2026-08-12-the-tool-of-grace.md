# 🔑 A Gift That Became a Workshop.

There are gifts that make life a little easier.

There are gifts that solve an immediate problem.

And then there are gifts that become **tools for building something much bigger than themselves.**

Back in April, two generous Christians placed a Lenovo laptop into our hands.

They did not ask for recognition.

They did not ask for their names to be published.

They simply saw a need and decided to help.

Because of that, this article will respect their privacy.

But privacy does not mean silence.

**We can still say thank you.**

And today, we want to show them what happened to that gift.

<!-- PLEBVOX:START -->

## 🔑 From Juelz And Me, Thank You.

Juelz and I are genuinely grateful.

Not simply because we received a laptop.

We are grateful because somebody looked at what we were trying to accomplish and decided that we were worth investing in.

That means something.

A great deal, actually.

The computer arrived at a time when having a capable machine could make an enormous difference to the work we were doing.

Instead of allowing that generosity to become merely another possession, we put the machine to work.

And it has been working ever since.

<!-- PLEBVOX:START -->

## 🔑 What Happened To The Computer?

I kept the existing **Windows 11 Pro** installation.

Alongside it, I installed **MX Linux 23.6, Libretto**, giving the machine a proper dual-boot environment.

The Linux installation runs **Xfce 4.20** on a **6.1-series Linux kernel**, with an Intel Core i7-8550U processor, 12 GB of RAM, Intel UHD Graphics 620, and a 240 GB KingMAX SSD.

In other words, that Lenovo did not sit on a shelf.

It became a serious little workstation.

MX Linux gives me a lightweight, stable environment in which I can write, research, develop software, manage files, experiment with Linux, build PlebWare, and continue learning.

Windows remains available when I need it.

Linux gives me the freedom to build.

**Two operating systems. One machine. A great deal of work.**

## 🔑 What Has The Gift Made Possible?

This computer has become part of the machinery behind PlebWare.

It is being used for:

* **Writing, articles, stories, and documentation.**
* **Research, study, and building a personal knowledge base.**
* **PlebMachine development and Linux experimentation.**
* **PlebVox development and accessibility work.**
* **Graphics, image preparation, and creative projects.**
* **Website development and GitHub work.**
* **Organising projects, documents, backups, and archives.**
* **Working with AI tools as part of the writing and development process.**
* **Learning new technologies and finding practical ways to make them useful.**

Some of the things created on this machine will eventually be read by people I will never meet.

Some may help somebody solve a problem.

Some may simply make somebody smile.

And some may give somebody who has very little access to technology the encouragement to realise that **they can create something too.**

## 🔑 This Is Where PlebWare Gets Interesting.

Quite the opposite.

**“What can an ordinary person build with the tools they actually have?”**

That Lenovo became another answer to that question.

It does not need to be the newest computer in the world.

It does not need the most expensive operating system.

It simply needs to be capable enough to help us turn ideas into reality.

That is precisely what it has done.

A donated computer became a Linux workstation.

The Linux workstation became part of a development environment.

The development environment became part of PlebWare.

And PlebWare continues becoming a place where knowledge, creativity, technology, faith, and ordinary everyday experience can meet.
