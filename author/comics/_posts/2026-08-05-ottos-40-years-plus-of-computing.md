# 🖥️ Otto's Journey: From VIC-20 to PlebWare

Every great journey begins with a single spark. Mine started with a humble **Commodore VIC-20**, where curiosity grew into a lifelong passion for programming, electronics, and solving problems.

Through decades of learning, setbacks, engineering, Linux, and open-source development, that passion evolved into **PlebWare**—a philosophy of practical, privacy-respecting, self-hosted technology built for real people.

This comic celebrates a lifetime of perseverance, proving that while the tools may change, the determination to build, learn, and create never does.

> **"My tools changed. My purpose didn't."** — Otto Brinkmeier
