## 🁢 The Dominoes Have Fallen
The first domino fell when the old bakkie broke down. 🚚💔

Otto stood beside it, staring at the open bonnet, his hands trembling against the weight of another disaster. His boss had no money to repair the vehicle, which meant no deliveries, no work, and no income.

One problem had become three. Then five. Then more than he could count.

It was as though life had lined up every little disaster and pushed the first domino with a cruel smile. 🁢🁢🁢

The electricity bill was overdue. The cupboards were nearly empty. The cleaning supplies were running out. Even the smallest necessities had become luxuries that demanded a decision.

Food or cleaning supplies?

Electricity or groceries?

Which essential could he afford to sacrifice next? 😞

He opened the cupboard and stared at the last few staples. There was barely enough to keep them going. His beloved pet waited nearby, looking up at him with trusting eyes.

That look hurt more than hunger.

"I'm sorry, old friend," he whispered. 🐾💔 "I'm trying. God knows I'm trying."

He sat down at the kitchen table and buried his face in his hands.

There were dishes to wash, pots and pans to clean, and almost nothing left in the household supplies. For three days, every drop of water, every spoonful of food, and every remaining resource would have to be stretched beyond what seemed reasonable.

The dishes would have to be rinsed and reused. The pots and pans would have to be cleaned with sand, carefully and sparingly, until proper supplies could be bought again.

### Three days.

Only three days until relief.

But when a person has reached the end of his strength, three days can feel like three years. ⏳😔

The walls seemed to close in around him. Every unpaid bill whispered an accusation. Every empty container reminded him of what was missing. The future, once a road full of possibilities, had become a dark corridor with no visible exit.

And the dominoes kept falling.

One breakdown. One lost income. One empty cupboard. One impossible choice after another.

There was no safety net beneath him. No reserve tucked away for a rainy day. No hidden account waiting to rescue him.

Only exhaustion. Desperation. And the crushing fear of what might happen if the next three days proved too much.

🌧️ Yet somewhere beneath the despair, a tiny ember remained.

Relief was coming. He knew that much. He did not have to solve the whole of tomorrow today. He had to survive this morning, then this afternoon, then another night.

He wiped his eyes, stood up, and looked at the dishes.

"One day at a time," he whispered.

It wasn't courage that made him move. Not exactly.

It was necessity.

He rinsed the first plate.

Then the second.

Outside, the world carried on as though nothing had happened. But inside that small home, a man was fighting a battle few people would ever see.

🕯️ Sometimes, survival isn't about standing tall. Sometimes, it's simply about refusing to lie down when every domino has fallen.

And sometimes, when all you have left is enough for three more days, getting through today is victory enough. ❤️‍🩹
