**🙏 Daily Devotion — Trusting God When It Gets Hard Too!**

**Sunday, 25 October 2026.**

**🙏 Opening Prayer.**

_Heavenly Father, I come before You in the precious name of Jesus Christ. You know the moments when our prayers seem unanswered and our circumstances feel too heavy to bear. Teach us to trust You when we cannot understand what is happening, and to stand firm when life becomes difficult. Help us not merely to hear Your Word, but to live it with courage, humility, and love. Strengthen the weary, comfort the hurting, and give hope to those who feel forgotten. If revival must begin with one person, Lord—let it begin with me. In Jesus' mighty name. Amen._

<!-- PLEBVOX:END -->

<!-- PLEBVOX:START -->

**📖 Today's Thought — When Heaven Seems Silent.**

Sometimes Christians reach a point in their walk with Christ when it seems as though God has become silent. We pray, but answers do not come when we expect them. Circumstances remain difficult, and we wonder how much longer we can keep going.

It is easy to speak about faith when life is comfortable. The real test comes when we have prayed, waited, and hoped, yet the situation has not changed.

This is not a call to pretend that pain does not exist. Scripture is full of honest cries to God. We may grieve, ask questions, and seek help. But we must be careful not to mistake God's apparent silence for proof that He has abandoned us.

When life gets hard, return to the foundations of your faith. Pray honestly. Stand firmly rooted in God's Word. Practise what you preach, and let what you believe shape the way you act.

Faith does not mean that we control every outcome. It means that we continue to trust God while taking the next wise and faithful step. We may not understand the whole road, but we can ask the Lord for strength for today.

<!-- PLEBVOX:END -->

**🏛️ Church History — Corrie ten Boom: Faith Through the Darkness.**

Corrie ten Boom was a Dutch Christian who, with members of her family, helped hide Jewish people from Nazi persecution during the Second World War. Their work was discovered, and the family was arrested. Corrie and her sister Betsie were eventually imprisoned at Ravensbrück concentration camp. Betsie died there in December 1944. Corrie was released later that month because of a clerical error.

Corrie did not escape suffering because she had faith. She endured imprisonment and witnessed terrible cruelty. After the war, however, she travelled and spoke about Christ, forgiveness, and hope.

Her story does not provide an easy explanation for every tragedy. Instead, it reminds us that suffering need not erase our faithfulness. Even in darkness, a believer can continue choosing compassion over hatred and hope over despair, one day at a time.

**📜 Scripture One — God Is Our Refuge.**

> "I will say of the LORD, He is my refuge and my fortress: my God; in him will I trust."
> **Psalm 91:2, King James Version (KJV).**

**💭 Reflection.**

A refuge is a place of shelter; a fortress is a place of protection. Our security begins with God, not with the illusion that we can control every circumstance. This does not promise a life without hardship. It reminds us that we can turn to God for strength and hope while taking responsible steps through the difficulties before us.

**❓ Personal Question:** When trouble comes, do I turn first to fear, or do I turn to God in prayer and seek His wisdom?

<!-- PLEBVOX:END -->

<!-- PLEBVOX:START -->

**📜 Scripture Two — Be Doers of the Word.**

> "For not the hearers of the law are just before God, but the doers of the law shall be justified."
> **Romans 2:13, King James Version (KJV).**

**💭 Reflection.**

In context, Paul is addressing God's righteous judgement and the difference between merely knowing God's requirements and living in obedience. This verse should be read alongside the wider teaching of Romans about sin, grace, faith, and salvation; it is not a claim that we earn salvation by good works.

The challenge remains: do our actions reflect what we say we believe? We cannot preach forgiveness while deliberately refusing to forgive, or encourage trust while allowing fear to govern every decision. We will not live perfectly, but we can confess our failures, receive God's grace, and begin again.

**❓ Personal Question:** What teaching of Jesus do I need to put into practice in my present circumstances?

<!-- PLEBVOX:END -->

<!-- PLEBVOX:START -->

**📜 Scripture Three — Stand Firm in the Faith.**

> "Watch ye, stand fast in the faith, quit you like men, be strong."
> **1 Corinthians 16:13, King James Version (KJV).**

**💭 Reflection.**

The older phrase "quit you like men" means to conduct oneself courageously. Paul calls believers to vigilance, steadfastness, and strength. This does not mean that Christians must never feel weak or ask for help. Courage can include humility, prayer, wise counsel, and accepting support.

The next verse reminds us that everything should be done in love (1 Corinthians 16:14). Christian strength is not arrogance or harshness. It is faithfulness expressed through courage, love, and perseverance.

**❓ Personal Question:** What would standing firm in faith look like in one practical, loving action today?

<!-- PLEBVOX:END -->

<!-- PLEBVOX:START -->

**🛠️ Practical Tip — Take the Next Faithful Step.**

When life feels overwhelming, do not try to solve everything at once.

1. Pray honestly about what is troubling you.
2. Read a passage of Scripture slowly and consider what it teaches.
3. Separate what you know from what you fear might happen.
4. Choose one constructive action: seek advice, make a plan, apologise, or complete a task.
5. Speak to a trusted Christian or pastor when you need prayer or support.
6. Leave room for God's timing while doing what is right today.

Trusting God does not mean refusing practical action. Prayer and wisdom belong together.

<!-- PLEBVOX:END -->

**📋 Daily Exercise — Practise What You Believe.**

Take ten quiet minutes today. Write down one difficulty you are facing. Then write one truth from Scripture that can guide your response. Identify one action that puts your faith into practice, pray about it, and take that step.

At the end of the day, ask yourself: _Did I allow my circumstances to dictate my conduct, or did I allow God's Word to guide my response?_ Do not use the answer to condemn yourself. Use it as an opportunity to grow.

<!-- PLEBVOX:END -->

<!-- PLEBVOX:START -->

**🌅 The Heavenly View — God Sees Beyond This Moment.**

We see the problem in front of us; God sees beyond our limited perspective. This does not mean every difficulty will end exactly as we wish, or that we will understand every reason for our suffering. Christian hope rests in Jesus Christ, whose death and resurrection are the foundation of our faith.

You are allowed to weep, ask for help, and admit that you do not understand. But do not surrender hope merely because you cannot see the next chapter. Keep your eyes upon Jesus. Hold fast to His Word. Walk in love, and take the next faithful step.

The road may be hard, but you do not have to walk it alone.

<!-- PLEBVOX:END -->

**🙏 Closing Prayer.**

_Heavenly Father, thank You for Your Word and for reminding us to trust You when life becomes difficult. Please draw near to those who feel forgotten, strengthen those who are weary, comfort those who are grieving, and give wisdom to those facing hard decisions._

_Teach us not to measure Your faithfulness by the circumstances of a single day. Help us practise what we preach, forgive as we have been forgiven, and show the love of Christ in our words and actions. When we feel like giving up, give us courage for the next step. When we need help, give us humility to ask. When we have strength to spare, teach us to support someone else._

_Let our faith be more than words. May it be visible in our conduct, compassion, and perseverance. Keep our eyes fixed upon Jesus Christ, the Author and Finisher of our faith._

_If revival must begin with one person, Lord—let it begin with me._

_In the precious name of Jesus Christ, our Lord and Saviour. Amen._

<!-- PLEBVOX:END -->

**💙 Final Thought — Keep Trusting God.**

You do not have to understand everything to trust God, and you do not have to feel strong every day to stand firm. Bring your pain to God, return to His Word, seek wise counsel, and practise the faith you profess.

**When the road gets hard, do not give up on God. Let your faith become the foundation upon which you continue to stand.**
