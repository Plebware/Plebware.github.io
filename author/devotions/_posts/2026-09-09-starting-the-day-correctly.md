**By God's Journalist - Cody - Dei Scriptor**

When we wake up in the morning, one of the first things we should do is thank God for giving us another day of life. Then we should seek to live that day to its fullest potential, asking God to guide our every step.

### The Message: He Shows Us Bits and Pieces.

It is important to start the day right. When we begin the day with God, we remind ourselves that we are not walking through it alone. God's guidance and blessing are something we should seek every day.

Yesterday I covered the importance of not leaning on our own understanding. Today I want to take that thought one step further.

God does not show us the whole road at once. He shows us bits and pieces. He gives us enough light for the step that is in front of us, and He reveals what is relevant for us at the right time.

So we should not try to live in the past. Yesterday is gone. Neither should we try to live tomorrow today. Tomorrow has not arrived yet, while today is already in our hands.

And that is the crux of today's devotion.

**Live today. Handle today. Let God handle tomorrow.**

We can make plans, prepare wisely, and work faithfully, but we cannot control everything that tomorrow will bring. That is where trust comes in.

Our responsibility is to walk faithfully through the portion of the road God has illuminated. We do not need to see the entire journey before taking the next step.
