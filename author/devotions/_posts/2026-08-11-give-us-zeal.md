# 🔥 Stir Up the Fire.

## 🙏 Opening Prayer.

Father God, this morning I come before You in the cold, not only asking You to warm my body, but asking You to set my spirit ablaze again; where my zeal has grown cold, stir it up, where disappointment has buried it, uncover it, where weariness has weakened it, strengthen it, and where I have willingly cast it aside, forgive me and call me back to the fire of Your presence, that my life may once again burn for Your glory, in the mighty name of Jesus Christ, Amen.

# 🔥 Introduction — When the Fire Goes Cold.

It is Tuesday — Otto's Monday.

It is cold.

It is raining.

There is even the possibility of snow.

The grocery cupboard is bare.

Yesterday, what appeared to be a blessing arrived at the door, only to be discovered as something that had already gone bad.

Hope came wrapped in mold.

That is life sometimes.

You reach for what you thought was an answer, only to discover disappointment.

You expect provision and find frustration.

You expect warmth and find cold.

But this morning I don't want to sit staring at the cold.

I want to **stir up the fire.**

There is too much cold water being poured over the things of God.

Too much apathy.

Too much hesitation.

Too much spiritual sleep.

Too many people who once burned with passion for God, but somewhere along the road allowed that fire to become an ember.

And I know this because I have done it myself.

I don't need a bridle this morning.

I need a **spur.**

I need something that pushes me forward.

I need **ZEAL.**

Not zeal for recognition.

Not zeal for personal glory.

Not zeal to prove myself better than somebody else.

I want zeal for the **Glory of God.**

Where is your zeal?

Have you scorned it?

Have you pushed it aside?

Have disappointment quenched it?

Have hardship buried it?

Have exhaustion smothered it?

Have the circumstances around you become so cold that you have allowed your own heart to become cold with them?

Then today is a day to wake up.

# 📖 Scripture One — Be Zealous.

> **"As many as I love, I rebuke and chasten: be zealous therefore, and repent."**
>
> — Revelation 3:19

Jesus spoke these words to a church that had become spiritually lukewarm.

That is important.

He did not say this to people who had never known Him.

He said it to a church.

A church can become comfortable.

A believer can become tired.

A servant of God can become discouraged.

A person can still attend church, still read Scripture, still speak Christian words — and yet discover that the fire inside has become weak.

But Jesus does not merely expose the problem.

He gives the answer:

**Be zealous.**

Wake up.

Repent.

Return.

Burn again.

Zeal is not merely excitement.

Biblical zeal is a heart that has been awakened to the worth of God and therefore desires to honour Him.

Sometimes we need God to give us fresh circumstances.

But sometimes what we really need is a **fresh fire in the circumstances we already have.**

The rain may continue.

The cupboard may remain empty.

The problem may not disappear overnight.

But our hearts do not have to become cold because our circumstances are cold.

# 📖 Scripture Two — Stir Up the Gift

> **"Stir up the gift of God, which is in thee."**
>
> — 2 Timothy 1:6

Paul told Timothy to **stir it up.**

That is powerful language.

A fire sometimes needs attention.

It needs fuel.

It needs the embers disturbed.

It needs somebody to stop staring at the ashes and start stirring what remains.

Perhaps your spiritual life feels like that today.

Perhaps there isn't a roaring flame.

Perhaps all you can see are a few glowing embers.

Then don't despise the embers.

**Stir them.**

Pray again.

Read the Word again.

Worship again.

Serve again.

Repent again.

Give thanks again.

Open your mouth and tell God honestly:

**"Lord, I want my fire back."**

God can breathe upon an ember and make it burn again.

# 📖 Scripture Three — Do Not Grow Weary

> **"And let us not be weary in well doing: for in due season we shall reap, if we faint not."**
>
> — Galatians 6:9

There is another enemy of zeal:

**weariness.**

Sometimes we don't lose our zeal because we stopped loving God.

Sometimes we become tired from fighting the same battles.

We become tired of struggling.

Tired of waiting.

Tired of disappointment.

Tired of having to start again.

But Scripture reminds us that faithfulness has a harvest.

We may not see it today.

We may not see it tomorrow.

But God sees every act of obedience.

Every prayer.

Every sacrifice.

Every kindness.

Every time you chose truth instead of compromise.

Every time you got back up after falling down.

Don't confuse **delay** with **denial.**

And don't allow exhaustion to convince you that your labor for God is meaningless.

Keep going.

Stir the fire.

There is still a harvest ahead.

# 🕊️ Church History — When the Church Remembered How to Burn.

The history of Christianity contains many moments when the Church became comfortable, formal, or spiritually tired — and then God awakened His people again.

The early Christians lived with extraordinary conviction.

They worshiped Christ despite persecution.

They preached despite opposition.

They served the poor.

They carried the Gospel beyond the boundaries of their own communities.

Their circumstances were often terrible, but their zeal was remarkable.

Centuries later, the Church experienced other periods of renewal.

One particularly famous example came through the eighteenth-century revival associated with **John Wesley** and the Methodist movement.

Wesley preached throughout Britain, often traveling enormous distances to proclaim the Gospel.

His ministry emphasized personal conversion, disciplined Christian living, Scripture, prayer, practical holiness, and reaching ordinary people who were often neglected by established religious structures.

The revival did not begin because the weather was perfect.

It did not begin because everybody suddenly became comfortable.

It happened because people became convinced that **God was worth everything.**

That is the lesson.

Revival fire is not primarily about noise.

It is about **devotion.**

It is about hearts becoming dissatisfied with spiritual sleep.

It is about believers saying:

**"God, wake us up."**

The Church has always needed that prayer.

And perhaps we need it again.

# ✍️ Poem — The Fire Within.

## 🔥 Stir the Embers.

When winter winds are howling
And rain falls from the sky,
When cupboards stand empty
And weary hearts ask why,

When yesterday's expectation
Turns to disappointment's stain,
Do not surrender to the darkness,
Do not make the cold your flame.

There is an ember waiting
Beneath the ash and stone,
A spark that God has planted
That He has called His own.

So stir it with your prayer, Lord,
Stir it with Your Word,
Stir it with repentance
Until Your voice is heard.

I do not need a bridle,
I need a spur today.
I do not need complacence,
I need fire on the way.

Not fire for my glory,
Not fire to make me known,
But fire that burns for Jesus,
And Jesus Christ alone.

So though the world be freezing,
And though the road be long,
Let those who belong to Jesus
Rise up and once more be strong.

The rain may fall around us,
The night may seem severe,
But those who burn for Jesus
Have nothing left to fear.

So wake, O sleeping Christian.
Repent and seek the Lord.
Stir up the holy fire.
Stand firmly on His Word.

And when our earthly journey
At last has reached its end,
May heaven find us burning
For Christ, our faithful Friend.

# 🛠️ Daily To-Do — Stir the Fire

Today, don't merely read about zeal.

**Do something with it.**

### 🔥 1. Pray for fresh fire

Spend a few quiet minutes asking God:

**"Lord, where has my zeal become cold?"**

Don't make excuses.

Don't blame everybody else.

Let God search your heart.

### 📖 2. Read the three Scriptures again

Read:

* Revelation 3:19
* 2 Timothy 1:6
* Galatians 6:9

Then ask yourself:

**What is God asking me to stir up?**

### 🙌 3. Do one thing for God's glory.

Do something today that serves another person without seeking recognition.

A small act of obedience can become fuel for a larger fire.

### 🗣️ 4. Speak life

Encourage somebody who is discouraged.

You may discover that while you are trying to warm somebody else's heart, God is warming yours.

### 🚫 5. Refuse one cold-water thought.

When the thought comes:

*"What's the point?"*

Answer it with:

**"God is still worthy."**

# 💡 Practical Tips — Keeping the Fire Burning.

A spiritual fire needs tending.

Here are five simple habits:

**Keep Scripture close.**
Don't let your Bible become furniture.

**Pray honestly.**
God already knows when you're discouraged. You don't have to pretend.

**Watch what feeds your mind.**
Constant negativity can slowly extinguish spiritual enthusiasm.

**Stay around people who encourage faith.**
Fire spreads. So does coldness.

**Act when God prompts you.**
Delayed obedience can become forgotten obedience.

And remember:

**Zeal does not mean you never get tired.**

It means that when you become tired, you return to the One who gives strength.
