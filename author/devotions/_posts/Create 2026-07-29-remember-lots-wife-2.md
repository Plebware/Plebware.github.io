# Remember Lot's Wife
## A Warning for Every Believer

*"Remember Lot's wife."* — Luke 17:32

---

## Part 2: The Danger of Worldliness

[← Previous](#part-1) | [Next →](#part-3)

I believe there has never been a time when warnings against worldliness were more needed by Christ's church than today.

Every age has its own epidemic disease.

**The epidemic disease of our time is the love of the world.**

It is a pestilence that walks in darkness and a sickness that destroys at noonday (Psalm 91:6).

Thousands of baptized church members resist immorality and unbelief. They don't commit obvious sins. They look like good Christians.

Yet they become victims to the love of the world.

By and by, they give up the race and turn their backs on Christ.

---

### What Stops Them?

Have they found the Bible to be untrue?

Has Jesus failed to keep His word?

No, not at all.

They have simply caught the epidemic disease. They are infected with the love of this world. They have grown comfortable listening to half-truths and following a "feel-good" gospel.

They stopped heeding **the full counsel of God**.

---

### Patterns We See Today

How many children from Christian families begin well and end poorly!

How many married people appear strong in the faith until their children grow up — and then they fall away!

How many young women seem to love Christianity until they reach their twenties — and then lose it all!

How many pastors work hard for a few years, only to become lazy and indifferent from the love of this present world!

> *"No one having put his hand to the plow and looking back is fit for the kingdom of God."*
> — Luke 9:62

---

### The Warning Is Clear

**She looked back. She became a pillar of salt.**

The Lord Jesus singles her out as a warning to His church — not to the world, but to His own disciples.

He is saying: **It is possible to start well and finish poorly.**

It is possible to have every advantage and still be lost.

It is possible to walk with God's people but not walk with God.
