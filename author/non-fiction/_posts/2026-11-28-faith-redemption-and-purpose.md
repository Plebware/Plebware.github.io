# Chapter Seven — Faith, Redemption and Purpose. 🕊️

*One morning in Johannesburg, a late bus led to an encounter that Otto Brinkmeier would remember for the rest of his life.*

There are moments in life when something happens that cannot easily be explained through ordinary circumstances. An unexpected event can become a defining part of a personal journey.

For me, one such moment happened around 1998, while I was working at Benrose.

I was already a qualified tradesman by then. My apprenticeship years were behind me, and I was going about the ordinary business of getting to work each day. I had no idea that one particular morning would become an experience I would remember for the rest of my life.

It was a morning that strengthened my understanding of obedience, deepened my relationship with Jesus Christ, and reminded me that God's love can reach someone through the simplest act of compassion.

## 🌱 The Foundations of Faith.

Faith was not something that suddenly appeared in my life in 1998.

My Christian background had earlier foundations. My father was Lutheran, and Christianity was part of the environment in which I grew up. I was confirmed in the Methodist Church in 1978, and my journey of faith continued through the years that followed.

Around 1979, following a conversation with a Christian acquaintance who challenged me about my relationship with Christ, I examined my faith more seriously and reaffirmed my commitment to Him in a personal way.

But there is a difference between knowing about Jesus Christ and experiencing a personal relationship with Him.

As I grew older, my understanding of faith developed. During my railway working years, Jesus Christ became especially important to me on a personal level. Faith became less about simply belonging to a church or knowing religious teachings, and more about trusting God and allowing Him to influence the way I lived.

Looking back, I can recognise that my relationship with Christ developed through different stages of my life. The encounter I am about to describe became one of the experiences that confirmed the importance of listening and responding when I felt the Holy Spirit prompting me.

For more of the earlier milestones in my spiritual journey, see [When Gratitude Leaves Us Speechless — A Journey of Grace](https://plebware.github.io/author/devotions/2026/10/31/when-gratitude-leaves-us-speechless/).

## 🚌 A Morning That Began Like Any Other.

At the time, I was working at Benrose and travelling to work by bus.

My daily journey involved two buses.

The first was the Number 18, which took me into town. From there, I would catch the Number 2 bus towards Malvern. I boarded the second bus around the Standard Bank building.

It was a familiar routine. Nothing particularly unusual about it.

But one morning, the Number 2 bus was running late.

I remember looking at my watch and wondering where the bus was. It was already about five minutes behind schedule.

While I was standing there, something happened that I can only describe as an impression placed upon my heart.

I felt the Holy Spirit telling me that I needed to walk up the road because there was somebody I needed to speak to.

It was an unusual instruction. I had never experienced anything quite like this before.

I could have ignored it. I could have remained at the bus stop, concentrating on getting to work on time.

Instead, I decided to obey.

I turned away from the bus stop and walked approximately two blocks in the direction of Forest Hill.

I had no idea who I was supposed to meet or what I was supposed to say.

I simply walked.

<!-- PLEBVOX:END -->

<!-- PLEBVOX:START -->

## ❤️ The Man Around the Corner.

As I reached the next part of the road, a man came around the corner.

Immediately, I felt that this was the person I had been sent to speak to.

I approached him.

Now, I must explain something. I had never done anything like this before. Walking up to a complete stranger and delivering a message about God was not something that came naturally to me.

But the words were already forming in my mind and heart.

I said to him:

"Hey bro, I've never done this in my life before, but I feel that God's telling me I must tell you that God loves you."

I wasn't delivering a prepared sermon. I wasn't trying to impress him with religious knowledge.

I was simply passing on what I believed God had placed on my heart.

And then something happened that I will never forget.

The man broke down in tears.

He told me that he was on his way to commit suicide.

He then pulled out a gun and showed it to me.

He told me that he believed God had sent me.

Suddenly, I understood that this was no ordinary conversation. This man was in a desperate situation, and the message I had been compelled to deliver had reached him at a moment when he desperately needed to hear it.

I spent approximately five minutes praying with him.

We eventually parted ways.

I never knew what happened to him afterwards, but I have never forgotten that encounter.

## 🙏 The Bus That Was Fifteen Minutes Late.

After parting ways with the man, I returned to the bus stop.

I estimated that I had spent about ten minutes away from the stop. Added to the original delay, the bus was now almost fifteen minutes late.

My first thought was that I was going to be late for work.

I had responsibilities, and I knew that arriving late was not ideal.

Then, as if the morning had one more surprise waiting for me, the very bus I had been waiting for finally arrived.

The driver was in a hurry. He urged the passengers to get aboard quickly because he was trying to make up lost time.

We set off, and the bus travelled considerably faster than usual.

And here comes the part that still makes me smile.

Despite the original delay, despite walking away from the bus stop, and despite spending time praying with a complete stranger, I arrived at work five minutes early!

I had been concerned about losing time, yet somehow I had arrived earlier than expected.

I have never forgotten the irony of that morning.

## 🔥 Obedience Before Understanding.

When I look back at that experience, I don't see it simply as an unusual coincidence involving a late bus.

I see it as a lesson in obedience.

I didn't know who I was going to meet. I didn't know what circumstances that person was facing. I certainly didn't know that he was contemplating taking his own life.

All I knew was that something was urging me to leave the bus stop and speak to somebody.

I had a choice.

I could have dismissed the impression as an odd thought, or I could act upon it.

I chose to act.

That morning taught me something about faith that goes beyond words and religious discussions.

Sometimes, we may not understand why we are being prompted to do something. We may not see the bigger picture, and we may not know what difference our actions could make.

But an act of kindness, a few sincere words, or a willingness to stop and listen can mean more to another person than we could ever imagine.

I believe that, on that morning, I was given an opportunity to be an instrument of God's love.

Not because I was particularly special, or because I had some extraordinary ability, but because I was willing to respond.

## 📖 A Message That Still Matters.

One of the most powerful messages in Christianity is that God's love is not reserved for the perfect, the successful, or those who have everything figured out.

God's love reaches people in their darkest moments.

Romans 8:38–39 reminds us that nothing can separate us from the love of God in Christ Jesus our Lord.

That is a message worth remembering.

We do not always know what another person is carrying inside. Someone standing beside us at a bus stop, sitting next to us at work, or passing us in the street may be fighting a battle we know nothing about.

We should never underestimate the value of compassion.

Sometimes, the most important thing we can tell another human being is simply that they are loved.

## ✍️ From Faith to Purpose.

This encounter became one of the important experiences in my personal journey with Christ.

It helped reinforce my understanding that faith should not remain something we keep to ourselves. It should influence how we treat people, how we respond to those in need, and how willing we are to share the message of God's love.

Years later, another experience would lead me towards Christian writing and the creation of short devotions.

That was a different chapter in my life, with its own circumstances and its own calling. While I was working as a shop driver and buyer, periods of waiting in a parked vehicle became the setting for a later prompting to write short devotions and encourage people. That story is told in [From Bored to God's Journalist](https://plebware.github.io/author/comics/2026/08/06/from-bored-to-gods-journalist/).

The circumstances were different, but I can see a connection between the two experiences.

In 1998, I was prompted to speak to someone who needed to hear that God loved him.

Years later, I would feel prompted to put messages of faith into writing so that they could reach people I might never meet.

The underlying message remained the same:

**Share the love of God. Encourage others. Remind people that they are not forgotten.**

That is something I still believe in today.

## 🕊️ Faith, Redemption and Purpose.

I cannot tell you what became of the man I met that morning. I don't know where life took him after we parted ways.

But I sincerely hope that he found the help, support, and peace he needed.

What I do know is that our paths crossed at a moment when he was in desperate need of hope.

And I was given the opportunity to tell him something simple:

**God loves you.**

For me, that is what this story is ultimately about.

Faith is not merely something we talk about. It is something we live.

Redemption reminds us that no person should ever be regarded as beyond the reach of God's love.

And purpose is sometimes found in the smallest moments of obedience, when we put aside our own plans long enough to recognise someone else's need.

I still think about that late bus.

I still remember the walk away from the bus stop.

And I still remember the man who broke down in tears when he heard those words.

Perhaps the greatest lesson I took from that morning was this:

**We may never know how far a simple act of obedience can reach. But we should never underestimate what God can do through a willing heart.**
