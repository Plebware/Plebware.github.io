# Otto Goes Naked

## **Introduction**

It is often said that I rely too much on Artificial Intelligence (AI), so I've decided to take a break from it for a few days. What I want to share is my perspective on what AI is, why I use it, and why it's not as bad as people make it out to be.

<!-- PLEBVOX:END -->
-----
<!-- PLEBVOX:START -->

## **Why I Use AI**

I've been using AI since 2024, when I started using Grammarly and Google services. I've found it to be a useful tool for my writing and other activities.

<!-- PLEBVOX:END -->
-----
<!-- PLEBVOX:START -->

## **What is AI?**

Artificial Intelligence is not as new as people think. It has been around since the 1950s, and it is becoming increasingly common. I've understood the concept of AI for a long time, and I've always believed that it would play a significant role in the future.

<!-- PLEBVOX:END -->
-----
<!-- PLEBVOX:START -->

## **Types of AI**

AI is a broad field that encompasses various types of intelligent systems, including rule-based systems, expert systems, and neural networks. These systems can perform tasks that typically require human intelligence, such as learning, problem-solving, and decision-making.

<!-- PLEBVOX:END -->
-----
<!-- PLEBVOX:START -->

## **Benefits of AI**

AI has the potential to save time, automate tasks, and improve productivity. However, it's essential to consider the potential risks and challenges associated with its development.

<!-- PLEBVOX:END -->
-----
<!-- PLEBVOX:START -->

## **The Dark Side of AI**

Some people worry that AI could become so advanced that it surpasses human intelligence, leading to errors and mistakes. While I agree that AI is not infallible, I believe that AI will not become sentient in the same way as humans.

<!-- PLEBVOX:END -->
-----
<!-- PLEBVOX:START -->

## **The Future of AI**

I'm excited about the potential of AI to improve our lives and make our work easier. I believe that AI will be a game-changer, and I'm eager to see where it will take us.

<!-- PLEBVOX:END -->
-----
<!-- PLEBVOX:START -->
## **Conclusion**

In conclusion, AI is not as bad as people think it is. While it has its limitations, I believe that it has the potential to improve our lives and make our work easier. I'm excited to see where AI will take us, and I'm confident that it will be a positive force in the world.

**🤖💻**

<!-- PLEBVOX:END -->
-----
<!-- PLEBVOX:START -->

## **Artificial Intelligence Evil Or Useful?**

It is both useful and good; by itself, it is not evil, and perhaps its greatest benefit is saving time. It is not infallible, not by a long shot, and it does make mistakes; neither is it sentient in the true sense. I also believe that it will never achieve sentience that is on par with human sentience.
Sadly, there is a danger in creating Artificial Intelligence that makes decision-making choices in military and law enforcement situations, exactly because Artificial Intelligence does not have emotions nor has a spirit that guides them, however I believe that the Artificial Intelligence that is publicly accessible will be safe to use for a short time still, probably for a long time.
That said, the benefits far outweigh the negatives by far, and I firmly believe that I will be one of the first people to warn you otherwise.
