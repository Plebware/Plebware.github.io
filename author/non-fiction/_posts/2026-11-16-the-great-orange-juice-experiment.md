# Squeezing the Truth Out of an Orange.

*An experiment with offline AI, fresh oranges, and a rather confused language model.*

## Introduction: A Little Experiment.

Sometimes the best way to test an AI assistant is to give it an ordinary everyday task.

This time, I asked my offline PlebAI Chat to write an article about squeezing fresh orange juice.

Nothing complicated. No rocket science. No quantum physics. Just a few ripe oranges and a glass of fresh juice.

What came back was rather entertaining!

## When AI Starts Making Things Up.

The AI began with an impressive-sounding introduction about maintaining healthy bones, elevated energy levels, natural defences, and avoiding stale fruit mixed with heavy cream.

Not bad for a glass of orange juice!

But things became considerably more interesting when it attempted to explain the squeezing technique.

Apparently, the correct approach involves:

- Using a one-litre plastic shaker.
- Applying a two-handed grip.
- Placing your thumb and forefinger around the rim.
- Using a sharp knife to cut through the cellulose fibres.
- Carefully controlling the pressure to preserve the natural sugars and vitamins.

I don't know about you, but I think I'll stick to an ordinary orange juicer!

## The Problem: AI Hallucinations.

This little experiment demonstrates something important.

AI systems can produce sentences that sound convincing, technically sophisticated, and grammatically correct while containing information that is completely inappropriate or simply incorrect.

This is commonly called an AI hallucination.

The problem isn't necessarily that the AI cannot write. The problem is that it can sometimes write confidently about things it doesn't actually understand.

And that is why human supervision remains essential.

## The Correct Way to Squeeze Fresh Orange Juice.

Let's get back to the oranges!

**What you need:**

- Fresh, ripe oranges.
- A manual citrus juicer or electric citrus press.
- A sharp kitchen knife.
- A glass or suitable container.
- A strainer, if you prefer juice without pulp.

**The method:**

1. Wash the oranges thoroughly.
2. Roll each orange gently against the kitchen counter to help release the juice.
3. Cut the orange in half across its middle.
4. Place one half onto the citrus juicer.
5. Press and twist until the juice has been extracted.
6. Repeat with the remaining halves.
7. Strain if desired, pour into a glass, and enjoy!

Fresh orange juice requires no complicated machinery, laboratory equipment, or one-litre shakers.

## The PlebAI Lesson.

This experiment was never really about oranges.

It was about testing the capabilities of an offline AI assistant and understanding why we must evaluate its answers rather than blindly accepting everything it produces.

PlebAI is a tool to assist us, not a replacement for common sense.

The human remains the decision-maker.

And sometimes, the best way to discover what an AI can and cannot do is to ask it something as simple as:

**How do you squeeze an orange?**

Just remember to keep the knife away from the shaker!
