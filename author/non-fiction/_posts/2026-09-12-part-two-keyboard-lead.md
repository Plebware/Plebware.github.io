# Part Two

## What Happens When I Let the Keyboard Lead?

Tonight I am going to try something different.

I am going to start writing without deciding what I am going to write about.

<!-- PLEBVOX:END -->

<!-- PLEBVOX:START -->

## Toothache and Victory or Death

Jullian is suffering from immense toothache, and as a result I ran across the road to source some Granpa pain powders.

Hopefully, that will help him.

My little outing made me look up at an EFF political poster, which made me flinch.

Boldly, at the bottom of the poster, there stood the words:

“Victory or Death.”

What a surreal moment.

Now that I am back at the keyboard, I am wondering what on earth the EFF is saying?

<!-- PLEBVOX:END -->

<!-- PLEBVOX:START -->

## No, I Refuse To Focus On Stupidity

That means no reflecting on Kakistocracies, Kleptocracies, nor any other political nuances.

Except perhaps a focus on plebeian humanitarian assistance.

That is what the PlebWare movement is supposed to be about.

Power for the common folk.

Apparently, my surname Brinkmeier was a name for someone who lived beside a meadow or grassy pasture.

You don't get more common than that.

I am a commoner, and it is only befitting that I become the founder of PlebWare.

<!-- PLEBVOX:END -->

<!-- PLEBVOX:START -->

## The Difference Between Thinking And Writing

Thinking often requires memory recall.

Humans like myself are somewhat limited when it comes to memory recall.

Writing, I perceive, is largely dependent on memory recall.

So the quality of authentic creative writing is directly proportional to genuine human input, and inversely proportional to the degree to which Artificial Intelligence replaces that input.

**Otto's Law of Creative Writing.**

*(Note: Not Heinrich's Law.)*

<!-- PLEBVOX:END -->

<!-- PLEBVOX:START -->

## Electrical Trade Theory's Influence On The Pen

As seen above in Otto's Law of Creative Writing, Ohm's Law had an effect.

Be it directly proportional or inversely proportional, it nevertheless had an effect.

Would you not say?

I am always game for a challenge, and what started out as a challenge to write without any Artificial Intelligence is actually turning into an experiment of partnering with Artificial Intelligence.

<!-- PLEBVOX:END -->

<!-- PLEBVOX:START -->

## Down The Rabbit Hole, And Poof, We Are In The Land Of Oz

Okay, that is cheesy.

But I did write an article about the history of computing through the eyes of Alice.

I just read *The Wizard of Oz* — no thanks to *Raid: Shadow Legends* implementing Dorothy and the Tin Woodman, the Cowardly Lion, the Scarecrow and the Wicked Witch Ozilda.

The question now is, what will I do with this?

Perhaps I should submit another article to *Everyday AI Magazine*, about how to work with Artificial Intelligence Agents without getting the poor Tin Woodman all rusted up.

<!-- PLEBVOX:END -->

<!-- PLEBVOX:START -->

## The End — Or Just The Beginning

Well, to conclude tonight's experiment, I will say we have reached the End.

But in reality, we are only really starting to find the beginning of a monumental journey.

The journey of the modern writer.

No longer just armed with a keyboard, but now also a microphone, via Portable PlebMachine, an effective AI Publishing Assistant, and a whole lot of determination.

So it is the end for now.

But tomorrow, we do this again.
