# 🚆 My Railway Years — Qualifications, Skills and Professional Development

During my years working in the South African railway environment, I acquired a number of professional qualifications and attended specialised training courses.

These records represent more than certificates hanging on a wall. They document the technical knowledge, practical abilities, safety awareness and leadership development that formed part of my professional journey.

The following is a chronological record of the qualifications and courses I have preserved.

<!-- PLEBVOX:END -->

---

<!-- PLEBVOX:START -->

## ⚡ 1. Electrical Trade Test — 7 June 1988

One of the most important milestones in my technical career was successfully completing my Electrical Fitter trade test, specialising in Electric Motive Power.

The examination was conducted through the Central Organisation for Trade Testing under the South African Department of Manpower.

My trade-test report records the practical and technical assessment undertaken during the examination, while the accompanying certificate confirms that I successfully passed the qualifying test.

**Qualification details:**

- Trade: Electrical Fitter.
- Specialisation: Electric Motive Power.
- Date tested: 7 June 1988.
- Certificate issued: 28 June 1988.
- Issuing authority: Department of Manpower, Republic of South Africa.

This qualification formally recognised my competence in the electrical trade and provided an important foundation for my subsequent railway work.

## 🚑 2. St John Ambulance — First Aid Certificate

**Issued: 7 February 1986 | Valid until: 7 February 1989**

Workplace safety is an important responsibility, particularly in an environment involving electrical equipment, heavy machinery and railway operations.

I successfully completed a First Aid examination through the St John Ambulance Association, under the Priory for South Africa of the Most Venerable Order of the Hospital of St John of Jerusalem.

The certificate records its issue date as 7 February 1986 and its validity until 7 February 1989.

This training added an important safety dimension to my professional qualifications, providing formal recognition of my First Aid examination.

## ⚙️ 3. Advanced Specialised 5M2A Electrical Training

**Date: 18 September 1992**

Railway electrical systems require specialised knowledge, particularly when dealing with motor coach electrical circuits and control systems.

I attended an advanced specialised electrical training course relating to the 5M2A motor coach.

The specialised training covered electrical sequencing and switching operations, including the operation of contactors through the sequence from the first notch to the fourth notch.

Understanding this sequence was important for working with the electrical control arrangements associated with motor coach operation.

**Course details:**

- Course: Specialised 5M2A.
- Training area: Electrical sequencing and switching.
- Subject: 5M2 motor coach operation.
- Technical focus: Contactor operation from first to fourth notch.
- Training centre: Braamfontein.
- Date: 18 September 1992.

This course represents an important part of my specialised railway electrical training.

## 🤝 4. TeamBuilder Leadership Course

**21–25 January 1996**

Technical knowledge is only one part of professional development. Working effectively with other people, communicating clearly and understanding team responsibilities are equally important.

Between 21 and 25 January 1996, I successfully completed the TeamBuilder course, presented by Inter-Lead.

This course highlighted my leadership abilities and added another dimension to my professional development.

It represented a move beyond purely technical competence towards the interpersonal and organisational abilities required when working with and contributing to a team.

**Course details:**

- Course: TeamBuilder.
- Provider: Inter-Lead.
- Duration: 21–25 January 1996.
- Achievement: Successful completion.

The certificate remains a reminder that professional development is not restricted to technical skills. Leadership and teamwork also have an important place in the workplace.

## 📜 Looking Back — A Record of Professional Development

Looking at these certificates together, I can see a progression in my professional development.

It began with formal electrical trade qualification, continued through workplace safety and specialised railway electrical training, and eventually included leadership and team development.
