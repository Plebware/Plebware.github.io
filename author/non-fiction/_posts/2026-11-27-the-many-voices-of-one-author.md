# Chapter Six — The Many Voices of One Author. 🎭

*One person can have many interests, many ways of expressing himself, and more than one name beneath which he writes.*

There is only one Otto Brinkmeier. The names that appear on his writing are not evidence of several people working behind the scenes. They are creative identities adopted by one person who enjoys exploring different subjects and expressing ideas in different ways.

Some readers may encounter Othello Cody Verrocchio on a work of fiction. Others may find the name Dei Scriptor beneath Christian writing, or Captain Gemini on a journalistic report. Then there are Lazar Mendicant and other occasional identities that have appeared as particular ideas, experiments, or pieces of writing have called for them.

These names are not disguises. They are creative choices.

<!-- PLEBVOX:END -->

<!-- PLEBVOX:START -->

## ✍️ The Tradition of the Pen Name.

Writers have used pen names for centuries. Sometimes a pseudonym offers privacy. Sometimes it helps an author distinguish between different genres, reach a particular readership, or create a recognisable literary identity. In other cases, a name simply feels more appropriate for the work being written.

There is a long tradition behind the practice, and Otto sees it as part of an author's freedom. A writer is not obliged to place every story, reflection, report, or experiment under one name.

For Otto, the choice is less about hiding and more about expression. A name can establish the tone of a piece before the reader has even begun. It can signal whether the work is imaginative fiction, Christian reflection, journalism, or something more playful.

This is one aspect of what writers call *author's licence*: the freedom to choose how a work is presented and how an authorial identity is expressed, while remaining honest about who is actually responsible for the work.

## 🌌 Othello Cody Verrocchio — The Literary Author.

Othello Cody Verrocchio is Otto's principal literary identity. It is the name he wants associated with his published fiction and, especially, his long-term science-fiction ambitions.

The identity gives his literary work a consistent name: one that can appear on a book cover, a story, or a fictional universe. It is associated with imaginative storytelling, speculative technology, philosophical questions, and the exploration of humanity through fiction.

Otto has set himself the ambitious goal of publishing twenty-one science-fiction books. Othello is the authorial name under which that body of literary work is intended to grow.

Readers interested in the origins and background of this identity can explore [The Many Faces of Cody!](https://plebware.github.io/everyday/about-plebware/2026/09/20/othello-cody-verrocchio.html), which records the earlier development of Otto's creative names. His [Othello fiction archive](https://plebware.github.io/author/fiction/) provides a home for stories and imaginative writing.

## 🕊️ Dei Scriptor — The Christian Writer.

Christian writing has its own place in Otto's creative life. He previously used the combined identity “Cody — Dei Scriptor” and the English description “God's Journalist”.

Over time, he began to reconsider that wording. “God's Journalist” is a phrase used by other people too, and it does not provide the distinctive identity he wants. He has therefore decided to leave “Cody” out of this writing name and use **Dei Scriptor** on its own moving forward.

The Latin name is intended to express the idea of a writer or scribe in God's service. It reflects the purpose behind this work: to explore Scripture, share Christian reflections, communicate the Gospel, and encourage readers to think about how faith is lived.

This is a change in the name he prefers to use, not a rejection of the writing that came before it. Older articles and archive pages may still carry the earlier wording. The new preference is simply clearer: Dei Scriptor.

## 📰 Captain Gemini — The Journalistic Voice.

Captain Gemini is the identity Otto uses for journalistic-style writing, reports, and observations. It gives that work a recognisable voice, particularly when documenting events, conversations, developments, and the occasional unusual day.

A report has a different purpose from a science-fiction story or a devotional reflection. It is concerned with what happened, what was observed, and what can responsibly be said about it. The Captain Gemini identity helps distinguish that style of writing.

One example is [Captain Gemini's Field Report: A Meeting of Minds](https://plebware.github.io/everyday/news/2026/10/21/otto-and-greg-conference-table-artificial-intelligence/), which records a conversation that moved from an ordinary workshop visit into questions about education, science, and artificial intelligence.

The name is a creative byline. It does not mean that another reporter is secretly doing the work. Otto remains the person behind it.

## 🎭 Lazar Mendicant and the Other Voices.

Not every identity needs to become a permanent author brand or fit neatly into a formal category.

Lazar Mendicant is one of the more experimental names. Other identities, including Dei Interretialis and names used for particular interests or humorous pieces, have appeared along the way. Some may return; others may remain attached to a single experiment.

That is part of the enjoyment. A name can be a character, a playful label, or a way to approach a subject from a different angle. It can give an idea room to develop without requiring Otto to turn every experiment into a permanent part of his public identity.

The names are not a claim that Otto has mastered every subject they represent. They are creative signposts, and the work itself must still stand on its own merits.

## 🖥️ Variety Is the Spice of Life.

There is another reason Otto enjoys having different writing identities: he simply likes variety.

He has never been particularly fond of seeing exactly the same computer wallpaper every day. He enjoys changing the appearance of his desktop and creating different environments. A familiar machine can feel fresh when the surroundings and working atmosphere change.

That preference is also reflected in [PlebMachine](https://plebware.github.io/PlebMachine/), his project for creating distinct desktop working environments. Its purpose goes beyond decoration, but the idea of moving between different modes and environments fits something Otto has enjoyed for a long time: adapting the space around him to the kind of activity he wants to do.

The same instinct appears in his writing. Fiction, Christian reflection, journalism, technical work, and playful experiments do not all need to sound alike. They can have their own atmosphere and their own voice.

“Variety is the spice of life” is a familiar saying, and it captures the point rather well. Otto enjoys having different environments in which to think, work, and create.

## 🧩 One Person, Several Voices.

The different names may look like separate identities when encountered across websites, archives, or articles. But the person behind them is the same.

Otto Brinkmeier is the real person. Othello Cody Verrocchio is his literary author identity. Dei Scriptor is the Christian writing name he now prefers. Captain Gemini is his journalistic voice. Lazar Mendicant and the other occasional names give him room for experimentation.

The boundaries are useful, but they are not walls. A story may raise a spiritual question. A technical project may inspire an article. A journalistic observation may become the starting point for a wider reflection. Creative work naturally crosses boundaries.

The names help readers understand the kind of work they are encountering. They also give Otto the freedom to explore different interests without pretending that he must be only one kind of writer.
