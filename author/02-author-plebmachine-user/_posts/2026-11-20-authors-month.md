# ✍️ Author's Month – Your Imagination Has a Home!

Hello fellow authors, writers, storytellers and scribes!

Have you ever wanted to write a book, publish your own articles, start a blog, explore journalism or simply put your thoughts and experiences into words?

Perhaps you're already an established author. Perhaps you're an aspiring novelist with a folder full of unfinished manuscripts. Or perhaps you're simply somebody who enjoys writing.

**Then this invitation is for you!**

PlebWare is introducing **Author's Month**, an opportunity to bring writers together, explore creative possibilities and discover how technology can make the business of writing a little easier.

## 🌍 First Things First – Welcome to The Circle!

We believe that writing should bring people together.

That's why we'd like to invite you to join our WhatsApp writing community, **PlebCircle – The Circle.**

It's a place where writers can meet, exchange ideas, ask questions, share experiences and learn from one another.

However, before joining, there is one important requirement.

### 🔐 A Small Introduction, Please!

**Every prospective member must introduce themselves when joining.**

We are building a genuine community of people who share an interest in writing. We want to know who we're talking to, where they're joining us from and what brings them into the world of writing.

Please provide three simple details:

1. **Your name:** At least a first name or the name you'd like us to call you. A surname is not required. Names such as AmJo, AmJack or simply Joe are perfectly acceptable.
2. **Your location:** Tell us where you're from. Your city, province, state or country is sufficient.
3. **Your writing discipline:** Are you a novelist, blogger, journalist, newspaper reporter, poet, technical writer, researcher, screenwriter or aspiring author?

Here are a few examples:

- 👋 "Hi, I'm Joe from Johannesburg, South Africa. I'm a science-fiction writer."
- 👋 "Hello, I'm Yvonne from Moscow, Russia. I write blogs and articles."
- 👋 "Hi everyone, I'm Jack from California, USA. I'm an aspiring novelist."
- 👋 "Hello, I'm Mary from London, UK. I'm a freelance journalist."

That's all we ask!

### 🛡️ Why Is This Important?

Unfortunately, online communities sometimes attract scammers, fraudsters, spammers and automated accounts.

We want to discourage these unwanted visitors and protect the integrity of our community.

A simple introduction helps establish that there is a genuine person behind the WhatsApp account.

Members who fail to introduce themselves may not be accepted or may be removed from the community.

We respect your privacy. You do not need to provide your surname, identity documents, residential address or other sensitive personal information.

**We want to meet writers, not collect personal records!**

## 📚 What Exactly Is Author's Month?

Let's get something straight.

Author's Month isn't a computer course, a technical examination or an attempt to turn everybody into a computer expert.

It's an opportunity to explore the relationship between writing, creativity and technology.

PlebWare has a considerable collection of resources, applications, information and tools. We realise that newcomers might find the sheer amount of material a little overwhelming.

That's precisely why we're doing this.

**We want to make the journey into PlebWare easier, friendlier and more accessible.**

We'll introduce things gradually, demonstrate useful techniques and help writers discover resources they might not even know existed.

## 🖥️ Discover Your Digital Writing Desk!

Every writer needs a comfortable working environment.

Some prefer a traditional notebook and pen. Others work with a laptop, desktop computer or tablet.

Some authors use sophisticated publishing software, while others are perfectly happy with a straightforward text editor.

There is no single correct way to write.

During Author's Month, we'll explore subjects such as:

- 📝 **Writing Applications:** Discovering tools for everyday writing, editing and manuscript development.
- 📖 **Book and Manuscript Preparation:** Organising chapters, documents and larger writing projects.
- 🔎 **Research and Reference:** Finding, collecting and organising information.
- 🎨 **Graphics and Illustrations:** Exploring book covers, illustrations and supporting artwork.
- 🤖 **Artificial Intelligence:** Using AI for brainstorming, research assistance, editing and creative exploration.
- 🌐 **Blogging and Publishing:** Preparing articles and getting your work onto the internet.
- 📂 **Digital Organisation:** Keeping your documents, resources and projects organised.

You don't have to master everything.

Choose what interests you, explore at your own pace and ask questions whenever you need assistance.

## 🤖 Artificial Intelligence – Your Assistant, Not Your Replacement!

Artificial intelligence has opened an entirely new world of possibilities for writers.

It can help you brainstorm, develop characters, explore storylines, organise research, proofread manuscripts and improve your writing workflow.

It can even help you create illustrations, book covers and promotional material.

But there's an important distinction.

**AI can assist with the writing process, but it cannot replace your imagination, personal experiences or individual voice.**

The author remains responsible for the work.

Your ideas, your decisions, your creativity and your storytelling are what make your writing unique.

We'll explore how to use these technologies intelligently without surrendering the very thing that makes you a writer.

## 🤝 A Community of Writers Helping Writers!

One of the greatest advantages of joining The Circle is the opportunity to connect with other people who share your interests.

Imagine being able to discuss a troublesome chapter with another novelist, exchange blogging ideas, ask a journalist about their experiences or discover how another author approaches research.

That's the sort of environment we'd like to encourage.

You might be an experienced professional with decades of writing behind you.

You might be publishing your first article.

You might not have written anything beyond a personal journal.

**Your experience doesn't determine your welcome. Your willingness to participate does.**

We encourage constructive discussion, mutual respect, helpful contributions and a willingness to learn.

## 🌱 Technology Must Serve Humanity!

This is one of PlebWare's guiding philosophies.

Technology should simplify our lives, expand our opportunities and help us achieve things that might otherwise be difficult.

It should never become an obstacle between a person and their ambitions.

You shouldn't have to become a software engineer simply because you want to publish a book.

You shouldn't have to understand every application on your computer to become a productive writer.

And you certainly shouldn't feel intimidated by artificial intelligence.

PlebWare is about making technology more accessible, understandable and useful.

**The technology serves the writer. The writer serves the story.**
