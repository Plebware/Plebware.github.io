# Welcome to the PlebWare Author Sanctuary.

## The Purpose of This Website.

Firstly, let me make a confession about why I created this rather unique website.

**First Off:** I created it for myself and my spiritual son, Julian. Its original purpose was simple: convenience and accessibility.

I wanted a place where I could keep important information relevant to my writing efforts, as well as a place where we could organise and develop our shared creative projects.

As writers and creators, we accumulate an enormous amount of information. Ideas, research, references, tutorials, resources, notes and unfinished projects. Sometimes finding that one piece of information we need becomes more difficult than the actual work itself!

I wanted to change that.

I wanted a central place where useful information could be organised, accessed and revisited whenever needed.

**Secondly:** It was always intended to become a public resource for other writers, authors and creative individuals.

Why keep useful knowledge to ourselves when it could help somebody else?

That is the philosophy behind this section of PlebWare.

## A Sanctuary for Writers and Creators.

Writing is not simply about putting words on a page.

It involves research, imagination, discipline, experimentation and, occasionally, a considerable amount of frustration!

Whether you write fiction, science fiction, fantasy, historical novels, biographies, Christian literature, poetry or technical documentation, the creative process requires tools and resources.

And those tools don't necessarily have to be expensive.

Here, I intend to share information that can help writers and creators make better use of the technology available to them.

This includes:

* **Writing Tools:** Applications, editors and software that make writing easier and more productive.

* **Research Resources:** Useful websites, reference materials and research techniques.

* **Linux for Authors:** Making Linux a practical and affordable working environment for writers.

* **Creative Software:** Applications for graphics, illustrations, publishing and other creative activities.

* **Organisation:** Managing research, manuscripts, notes, references and ongoing projects.

* **Artificial Intelligence:** Exploring how AI can assist writers without taking away their creativity or independence.

* **Practical Tutorials:** Guides, instructions and lessons based on actual experience.

This is not intended to be an exhaustive catalogue of everything available on the internet.

Rather, it is a growing collection of resources, experiences and discoveries that I believe are worth sharing.

## Why Linux?

One of the reasons I created PlebWare is my belief that a computer should serve its user, not the other way around.

Linux offers an opportunity to build a capable creative workstation without necessarily investing in expensive software licences or proprietary ecosystems.

There are some exceptionally capable applications available within the Linux community.

From writing and research to graphics, video editing and desktop publishing, there is a great deal that can be accomplished using freely available software.

I use MX Linux as my primary working environment, and much of what I share here will reflect my experiences with Linux.

However, this does not mean that everything here will be exclusively Linux-related.

Writers use different operating systems, different applications and different workflows.

The objective is to share useful knowledge, not to insist that everybody work the way I do.

## PlebMachine: A Different Approach to Computing.

PlebMachine is another important part of this journey.

It is a project that has been close to my heart for many years. Its purpose is to make the computing experience more convenient, organised and accessible.

The philosophy is straightforward:

**Your computer should serve you — not the other way around.**

PlebMachine is being developed around the idea that different people have different computing needs.

A beginner should not be overwhelmed by unnecessary complexity, while an experienced user should not be restricted by an environment designed exclusively for beginners.

The objective is to provide an environment that can adapt to the user's needs while leaving the user firmly in control.

I will share information about PlebMachine, its development, its applications and the practical lessons learned along the way.

For authors, this is particularly relevant.

A well-organised computer can make the difference between spending your time creating something and spending your time trying to find the tools and information needed to create it.

## A Place to Share Experience, Not Just Information.

There is something I have learned over the years.

You don't necessarily have to be an expert to help somebody else.

Sometimes, the most valuable information comes from somebody who has simply encountered a problem, worked through it and documented the solution.

That is the approach I want to take here.

I will share my own experiences, successes, mistakes and discoveries.

Some articles will be tutorials. Others will be recommendations, observations or discussions about the tools we use.

Not everything will be perfect, and not every method will suit everybody.

But I believe that sharing practical experience is one of the best ways we can help one another.

If something works, I will tell you.

If something doesn't work, I will tell you that too!

## A Word About Creativity and Artificial Intelligence.

We live in an interesting period for writers and creators.

Artificial intelligence has introduced possibilities that were difficult to imagine only a few years ago.

I personally use AI as a creative assistant, research companion and technical collaborator.

But I believe there is an important distinction that should never be forgotten:

**Artificial intelligence should assist human creativity, not replace human thought.**

The ideas, imagination, experiences, decisions and ultimate responsibility belong to the human creator.

Technology can help us overcome obstacles, explore possibilities and become more productive.

But the individual should always remain in control of the creative process.

That principle applies to PlebMachine as much as it applies to writing.

## An Invitation to Fellow Writers.

Whether you are an established author, an aspiring novelist, a hobbyist, a blogger or simply somebody who enjoys creating things, you are welcome here.

You don't need to be a professional.

You don't need expensive equipment.

You don't need to know everything about computers.

All you need is the willingness to learn, experiment and create.

My hope is that something you find here will make your own creative journey a little easier.

And perhaps, in the process, you might discover something that you can share with somebody else.

After all, knowledge becomes considerably more valuable when we share it.

## Finally: Why Call It a Sanctuary?

Because I would like this to be a place where creativity is encouraged rather than judged.

A place where curiosity is welcome, where mistakes are part of learning, and where people can explore their creative ambitions without feeling that they have to compete with everybody else.

Writing can sometimes be a lonely occupation.

Having somewhere to find encouragement, useful information and practical assistance can make a difference.

This is my little contribution to that world.

Welcome to the PlebWare Author Sanctuary.

Pull up a chair, explore, learn something new, and above all...

**Keep Creating!**
