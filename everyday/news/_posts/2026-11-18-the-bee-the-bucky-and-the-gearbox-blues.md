# 🐝🚙 The Bee, the Bucky, and a Wednesday That Went Wrong!

**📰 Captain Gemini — Fortsburg Field Report.**

📅 Wednesday, 18 November 2026.

📍 Fortsburg, Johannesburg.

Some days just refuse to cooperate. And today, Otto discovered that even a routine stock run can come with an unexpected sting — followed by another round of negotiations with a Ford Ranger that apparently has opinions of its own!

Let's follow today's events. 🔎

<!-- PLEBVOX:END -->

<!-- PLEBVOX:START -->

## 📦🚚 From Yarona to China City!

Otto had dropped off the stock from Yarona and then had to return to pick up Ibram. Jade came along for the trip to China City, where there was another substantial load of stock waiting.

Otto was busy loading the stock when an entirely different kind of visitor made its presence known.

And this visitor didn't need a delivery note! 🐝

<!-- PLEBVOX:END -->

<!-- PLEBVOX:START -->

## 🐝 The Bee That Wanted a Piece of Otto!

While loading at China City, Otto rested his arm over the back of the Ford Ranger. When he lowered his arm onto the side of the vehicle, there happened to be a bee sitting exactly where his arm landed.

The result?

**STING!** 🐝

An unexpected encounter, and a rather personal reminder that bees are not particularly concerned about our plans for the day.

This all happened before twelve o'clock. Now, according to an old superstition, a bee sting before noon is supposed to bring good luck.

Pity Otto isn't superstitious. Because, judging by what followed, that good luck seems to have taken a wrong turn somewhere!

<!-- PLEBVOX:END -->

<!-- PLEBVOX:START -->

## ⚙️🚙 The Bucky's Gear Selector Still Has a Mind of Its Own!

Back in Fortsburg, the Ford Ranger — affectionately known as Bucky — was still causing frustration.

The gear selector continues to behave unpredictably. Occasionally, the selector gate and the gear positions simply don't line up as they should.

At times, Otto has to switch the vehicle off and manipulate the gear selector before the gate lines up again.

That is not a comfortable situation for someone who depends on the vehicle to do his work.

A driver should be able to select the gear he needs without having to stop, switch off, and coax the selector into position.

And this isn't a once-off irritation. It is an ongoing problem that has continued despite the money already spent on repairs.

<!-- PLEBVOX:END -->

<!-- PLEBVOX:START -->

## 🛠️ The Message: Drive It As It Is!

The latest response from the boss is that Otto must drive the Ranger as it is.

The explanation is that too much money has already been spent on the vehicle.

That leaves Otto in a difficult position. He is still expected to use the vehicle for work, while the gear-selection problem remains unresolved.

But money already spent on repairs does not, by itself, make a mechanical problem disappear.

The question is not whether the vehicle has already cost money. The question is whether it is working reliably and safely now.

<!-- PLEBVOX:END -->

<!-- PLEBVOX:START -->

## 📅🔧 December: Let Another Driver Experience It!

Otto is waiting for the boss's brother-in-law, who is also a co-owner of the shop, to arrive in December.

He has a driver's licence, and Otto intends to insist that he drives the Ranger for a week.

Not just a quick spin around the block. A whole week!

Otto wants another driver to experience the same gear-selection difficulties for himself — the selector that sometimes refuses to line up, and the occasions when the vehicle has to be switched off before the selector can be coaxed back into position.

There is a practical reason for this request: another driver may be able to confirm that the problem is with the vehicle rather than with the person behind the wheel.

Sometimes, the clearest way to understand a complaint is to experience it firsthand.

<!-- PLEBVOX:END -->

<!-- PLEBVOX:START -->

## 🤔📰 Captain Gemini's Closing Observation!

Let's recap the day's events.

🐝 An unexpected bee sting before noon.

📦 A stock run from Yarona, followed by more loading at China City.

🚙 A Ford Ranger that continues to make gear selection unnecessarily difficult.

⚙️ A selector that sometimes needs the vehicle switched off before it will line up properly.

💰 And the argument that too much money has already been spent.

Otto is not asking for a new vehicle or special treatment. He wants the Ranger to select its gears reliably so that he can get on with his work.

That is a reasonable expectation.

A mechanical fault does not disappear because everyone is tired of discussing it. And a driver should not have to accept an unreliable gear selector as simply part of the job.

<!-- PLEBVOX:END -->

<!-- PLEBVOX:START -->

## 🍀🐝 Perhaps Tomorrow Will Be Luckier!

They say a bee before noon brings good luck.

Perhaps the luck is still on its way. It certainly hasn't arrived in the form of a cooperative gear selector yet!

For now, Otto is back in Fortsburg, with a sore arm, another frustrating day behind him, and December marked as the next opportunity to have someone else experience what he has been dealing with.

And if Bucky keeps choosing its own gears, perhaps the next delivery should include a referee!

📰 **Captain Gemini — Reporting from Fortsburg.**

*Truth is stranger than fiction. And sometimes, it comes with wings and a troublesome gearbox.*
