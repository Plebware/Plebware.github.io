# 📰 The Reality of the Hunt.

Good morning. Captain Gemini here, reporting on behalf of the Brinkmeier and de Villiers clan.

This morning, I was fortunate enough to record our cat catching a mouse. At least he is more than capable of looking after himself. One less thing for the household to worry about!

Unfortunately, the same cannot be said for the rest of us just now.

This past week alone, I have lost R960 in income because I have been unable to work.

<!-- PLEBVOX:END -->

<!-- PLEBVOX:START -->

## 📡 Fibre, Dustbins, and the Real World.

We are still having problems with our fibre network, and I honestly don't get it. Using my neighbour's palm-sized Wi-Fi modem produces better latency than our fibre connection. That speaks volumes all by itself.

Sitting outside, I can hear the dustbins being ravaged for plastic, paper, and anything else that might be usable. It reminds me that I must take ours out.

Right. Back in a moment.

And I'm back. Dustbin is out, and now it's time for some wordsmithing action.

My apologies if my articles sometimes seem long and drawn out, yet laconic in content. Even my devotions are suffering. At times, it feels as though even God is being laconic.

Then a portion of Scripture comes to mind:

> “Be still, and know that I am God.” — Psalm 46:10 (KJV)

Perhaps stillness is precisely what I need while the dominoes keep falling.

<!-- PLEBVOX:END -->

<!-- PLEBVOX:START -->

## 💸 A Detailed Recap of the Financial Dilemma.

I'm almost too scared to do the maths in my head. The figures are heavy enough without having to calculate them all over again.

Here is how the week unfolded.

- **Last Wednesday:** I worked and was paid, although I had to sit by the mechanic and watch the company vehicle all day.
- **Wednesday evening:** We test-drove the vehicle and discovered that more work was still needed. We had to leave it with the mechanic.
- **Thursday:** No work, no pay.
- **Friday:** No work, no pay.
- **Saturday:** No work, no pay.
- **Sunday and Monday:** My usual days off.
- **Tuesday:** The vehicle still wasn't sorted. No pay.
- **Wednesday:** Another day without pay.
- **Thursday:** At the time of writing, it looks as though today may be another day without pay. The vehicle will certainly not be ready this morning.

I have heard nothing from the mechanic, and nothing from my employers, whose vehicle it is.

That makes **five unpaid working days** since last Wednesday, with the possibility of another lost day today. And this comes on top of the days I have already lost over the past two or three months.

No wonder I don't want to do the maths. It's heavy when one domino keeps falling after another.

<!-- PLEBVOX:END -->

<!-- PLEBVOX:START -->

## ⏳ Waiting on Alec.

I phoned Alec, the mechanic who has the company vehicle. He had not collected it, so I left a voice message and asked him to give me some feedback.

Now I wait.

I need to know what is happening so I can plan my day, or whatever remains of my week.

A friend has asked me to do some private work. I could perhaps go and tackle those little jobs, but they are struggling financially too, so the remuneration would probably be very little.

I'm not keen on taking it on under those circumstances. But when you need to make ends meet, you have to consider every possibility.

And that is the worst part of this holding pattern: I cannot properly plan my day until Alec gets back to me. The only other option on the table is low-paying private work for friends who are also trying to keep their heads above water.

<!-- PLEBVOX:END -->

<!-- PLEBVOX:START -->

## ⚙️ The Gearbox Is Back.

Then, at last, a little good news.

My employer phoned and told me:

“Uncle, they got the gearbox back. They're putting it in today, so maybe this evening we can go fetch it, or early this afternoon.”

He said he would let me know. So, once again, I have to remain on standby.

After five unpaid working days since last Wednesday, at least the gearbox is back and being fitted. That is something.

But I still cannot make firm plans. I must wait for the next phone call and see whether the vehicle is ready this afternoon or this evening.

<!-- PLEBVOX:END -->

<!-- PLEBVOX:START -->

## 🌤️ A Little Promise in the Waiting.

There is some promise in all of this.

The gearbox is back. Work is being done. And after a long wait, we finally have a little movement in the right direction.

It may not solve every financial problem, and it certainly doesn't bring back the income already lost. But it is a step forward.

For now, I remain on standby, hoping that the next call brings better news and that I can get back to work.

This is the latest field report on the Brinkmeier–de Villiers financial saga.

Captain Gemini, signing off.
