# **Who or What is PlebWare**
It is time to explain the when?; who?; where?; why? and what? regarding **_Plebware_** and **PlebWare**
Firstly, there are two deliberate ways to spell and pronounce **_Plebware_**.
Firstly, the Capital P, written as one word and pronounced as one word, represents the device operator or, as the mainstream says, the user.
Secondly, one word, but with both Capital P and Capital W, pronounced as two words, represents the Philosophy and the Ecosystem.
