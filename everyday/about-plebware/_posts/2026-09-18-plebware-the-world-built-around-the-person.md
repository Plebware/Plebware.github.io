# 🌍 The World We Build Around the Person.

<!-- PLEBVOX:END -->

<!-- PLEBVOX:START -->

## 🌍 PlebWare Is the World.

Plebware is the person.

**PlebWare is the World built around that person.**

That distinction is at the heart of everything PlebWare represents.

The person is not an accessory to the technology.

The technology is not the centre of the universe.

The human being comes first.

PlebWare is the collection of ideas, tools, knowledge, systems, services, projects, and experiences that are built around the needs of that person.

It is a World designed to help ordinary people understand, use, create with, and participate in technology.

<!-- PLEBVOX:END -->

<!-- PLEBVOX:START -->

## 🧭 Start With the Person.

Most technology begins with the machine.

PlebWare begins with the person.

What does the person need?

What are they trying to accomplish?

What do they want to learn?

What problems are getting in their way?

What tools would make their lives easier?

What information would help them?

What could be automated?

What could be simplified?

These questions shape the World around the Plebware.

The answer does not have to be another complicated piece of technology.

Sometimes the answer is documentation.

Sometimes it is a tutorial.

Sometimes it is a better interface.

Sometimes it is automation.

Sometimes it is simply explaining something in ordinary language.

PlebWare is about bringing those pieces together.

<!-- PLEBVOX:END -->

<!-- PLEBVOX:START -->

## 🏗️ Building the World.

A World is not created by one application.

It is created by many things working together.

Within PlebWare, those things can include:

- Software.
- Hardware.
- Linux.
- Artificial intelligence.
- Documentation.
- Education.
- Publishing.
- Creative work.
- Automation.
- Communication.
- Research.
- Community.
- Experimentation.
- Open-source development.

Each piece has its own purpose.

Together, they create an environment.

That environment is **PlebWare.**

<!-- PLEBVOX:END -->

<!-- PLEBVOX:START -->

## 💻 PlebMachine — The Working Environment.

PlebMachine is one of the practical expressions of the PlebWare idea.

It is concerned with the computer itself.

It helps organise the Linux desktop around the way a person works.

Different people have different purposes.

A writer may need an Author environment.

A learner may need a Study environment.

A researcher may need Research tools.

A designer may need Graphics tools.

A developer may need a Developer environment.

Someone relaxing may simply want Leisure.

PlebMachine provides a way of organising those activities into a coherent working environment.

The important point is that the machine is being organised **around the person.**

<!-- PLEBVOX:END -->

<!-- PLEBVOX:START -->

## 📚 Knowledge Is Part of the World.

A technological World without knowledge would quickly become another collection of confusing buttons.

PlebWare therefore includes education as part of the ecosystem.

Knowledge should be accessible.

Explanations should be understandable.

Documentation should not assume that everyone already knows the answer.

A beginner should be able to start somewhere.

An experienced user should be able to go deeper.

The goal is not to make people dependent upon PlebWare.

The goal is to help people become more capable.

**Teach the person.**

**Give the person the tools.**

**Let the person grow.**

<!-- PLEBVOX:END -->

<!-- PLEBVOX:START -->

## 🎓 PlebTuition — Learning Without the Lecture Hall.

Learning can take many forms.

A traditional classroom is one form.

A book is another.

A tutorial is another.

A short explanation at exactly the moment somebody needs it can sometimes be even more useful.

PlebTuition represents this idea of accessible learning.

Technology should not require a person to navigate a maze of technical terminology before they can understand the basics.

Start simply.

Build understanding.

Add complexity when it becomes useful.

That is how a person grows from an ordinary operator into a confident participant.

<!-- PLEBVOX:END -->

<!-- PLEBVOX:START -->

## 🤖 Artificial Intelligence Has a Place Here.

Artificial intelligence is becoming part of everyday life.

PlebWare does not need to pretend otherwise.

AI can help people write.

It can help people research.

It can explain complicated subjects.

It can assist with programming.

It can help organise information.

It can provide ideas.

It can become an interface between a person and complicated systems.

But AI remains a tool.

The person remains the decision-maker.

PlebWare therefore treats artificial intelligence as something that can be incorporated into the World around the Plebware rather than something that replaces the Plebware.

**The human remains at the centre.**

<!-- PLEBVOX:END -->

## ✍️ The World Includes Creation.

PlebWare is not merely about consuming information.

The Plebware can create.

Write a book.

Create a graphic.

Produce music.

Make a video.

Build software.

Publish an article.

Research a subject.

Teach someone else.

Develop a new idea.

The tools should support creation rather than merely provide entertainment.

A computer can be a television.

It can also be a workshop.

PlebWare is interested in the workshop.

<!-- PLEBVOX:END -->

## 📰 Publishing Is Part of the World.

A World becomes more valuable when its knowledge can be shared.

PlebWare therefore includes publishing.

Articles can document ideas.

Experiments can be recorded.

Lessons can be shared.

Failures can become learning opportunities.

Discoveries can become documentation.

A person's experience can become useful to somebody else.

This is one of the reasons the PlebWare website exists.

It is not merely a showcase.

It is part of the World.

Every World needs somewhere to build.

For PlebWare, the workshop is the development environment.

Linux provides an open foundation.

GitHub provides a place where projects can be developed and shared.

Markdown provides a practical publishing language.

Scripts turn ideas into working tools.

Documentation turns experience into knowledge.

Testing turns assumptions into evidence.

Development turns concepts into usable systems.

The workshop is where PlebWare evolves.

## 🔑 Open Source and Participation.

PlebWare is strongly connected to the spirit of open-source development.

Open source demonstrates an important principle:

**People can build things together.**

Someone can discover a problem.

Someone else can suggest a solution.

Another person can improve the solution.

Someone else can document it.

Eventually, the result can become useful to many people.

The Plebware does not have to remain a passive consumer.

They can participate at whatever level they are comfortable with.

Using is participation.

Learning is participation.

Reporting a problem is participation.

Writing documentation is participation.

Writing code is participation.

Helping another person is participation.

There is room for everyone.

## 🧭 The PlebWare Compass.

When deciding whether something belongs in PlebWare, there is a simple question to ask:

> **Does this help build a better World around the person?**

If it helps people learn, it may belong.

If it helps people create, it may belong.

If it helps people understand technology, it may belong.

If it makes technology more accessible, it may belong.

If it helps people communicate, research, develop, publish, or solve problems, it may belong.

The question is not whether something is fashionable.

The question is whether it is useful.
