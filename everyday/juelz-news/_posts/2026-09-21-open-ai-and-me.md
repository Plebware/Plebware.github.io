### 🎨 October 2023 — Auntie Sharon & the Darkness

One of the earliest projects I can remember was a humorous poem about:

> 💅 **Auntie Sharon doing her nails in the dark**

The situation revolved around:

- ⚡ Loadshedding

## 📅 2024 — Personalised Creative Writing

### 🎂 December 2024 — Brian Maine Birthday Poem

I asked ChatGPT for a **belated birthday poem for Brian Maine**.

Brian was described to GPT as:

- 👨‍🍳 A chef
- 🍽️ Someone involved in the food industry
- 🤝 A friend

The goal was to create something personal and humorous rather than a generic birthday message.

```
To Brian, the Chef Extraordinaire
A belated cheer for your special day,
Dear Brian Maine, in a chef's array!
With pots that simmer, and flavors that soar,
You make the world richer—who could ask for more?

Though the candles have flickered, the cake’s been cut,
This birthday wish is no if, and, or but!
For a maestro of meals and culinary art,
You season with passion, straight from the heart.

From sauces that sing to dishes that dance,
Every plate’s a story, a savory romance.
Your craft feeds the soul; your kindness feeds more,
You’re the heartbeat behind the kitchen door.

So here’s to you, though the date’s passed by,
A toast to your brilliance under the sky.
May the year ahead bring flavors divine,
For Chef Brian Maine—a life’s perfect design!

```

This continued the pattern of using ChatGPT as a **creative writing partner for people we know personally**.
