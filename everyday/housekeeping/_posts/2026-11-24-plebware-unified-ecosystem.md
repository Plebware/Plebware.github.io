# 🌐 One Philosophy. Twelve Categories. Three Environments.

**Technology Must Serve Humanity.**

PlebWare is developing as a connected ecosystem made up of three complementary environments: PlebWare, PlebMachine and Pleb Circle. Each has a different role, but all three share one philosophy and one common organisational structure.

This reference document defines that structure, explains the purpose of the twelve principal categories, and provides guidance for keeping the three environments consistent as the project grows.

<!-- PLEBVOX:END -->

## 🔑 The Guiding Principle.

**One Philosophy. Twelve Categories. Three Environments.**

The guiding philosophy is:

**Technology Must Serve Humanity.**

Technology should help people learn, create, communicate, solve problems and make informed decisions. It should remain understandable, useful and accessible. People retain control; technology exists to serve human purposes.

The twelve categories provide a shared way to organise knowledge, tools, projects and conversations. They are the common organisational backbone of the PlebWare ecosystem.

<!-- PLEBVOX:END -->

<!-- PLEBVOX:START -->

## 🟢 PlebWare — The Knowledge and Publishing Centre.

PlebWare is the knowledge, publishing and learning environment. It is the library and public reference point of the ecosystem.

Its purpose is to publish articles, tutorials, research, creative work, explanations and practical resources that people can read, revisit, learn from and share.

**Identity colour: Green.**

**Primary role: Knowledge.**

PlebWare is where information is organised, explained, documented and made available.

<!-- PLEBVOX:END -->

<!-- PLEBVOX:START -->

## 🔵 PlebMachine — The Practical Desktop and Technology Environment.

PlebMachine is the practical technology and productivity environment. It brings tools, workflows and computing activities together around the work a person wants to do.

Its purpose is to help people use their computers effectively, understand their tools, organise their work and accomplish practical tasks without unnecessary complexity.

**Identity colour: Blue.**

**Primary role: Tools and Productivity.**

PlebMachine is where people put knowledge and technology to practical use while remaining in control of their own systems and decisions.

<!-- PLEBVOX:END -->

<!-- PLEBVOX:START -->

## 🟣 Pleb Circle — The Human Community and Interaction Centre.

Pleb Circle is the community and communication environment. It brings people together to exchange ideas, ask questions, share experience, learn from one another and participate in the wider PlebWare project.

Its purpose is meaningful human interaction. It is not intended to be a passive announcement channel or a collection of silent observers.

**Identity colour: Purple.**

**Primary role: People and Participation.**

Pleb Circle is where the human connections behind the ecosystem take shape.

## 🧭 The Twelve Principal Categories.

The following twelve categories are the shared organisational structure used across the PlebWare ecosystem. Their descriptions define their intended scope. The live website repository remains the authority for the current category names, locations and published content.

### 1. Everyday.

Everyday is the practical home for ordinary life and everyday computing. It covers useful information, household and personal organisation, general technology use, practical guidance and the small tasks that help people manage daily life.

**Purpose:** Make everyday activities and technology easier to understand and manage.

### 2. Author.

Author is the home for writing, storytelling and the craft of words. It includes fiction, non-fiction, poetry, journalism, reflections, comics, cookbooks and devotional writing.

Writing and Devotions may be given separate discussion spaces where that helps people find the right conversation, while remaining within the Author category.

**Purpose:** Support written expression, creative work, publishing and the development of ideas.

### 3. Study.

Study brings together lessons, explanations, educational material and practical learning resources. It helps people build understanding, strengthen skills and explore subjects at their own pace.

**Purpose:** Turn learning into useful knowledge and practical understanding.

### 4. Research.

Research is for investigation, careful reading, source evaluation, comparisons, experiments and the pursuit of reliable answers. It encourages people to examine evidence and distinguish established information from assumptions or opinion.

**Purpose:** Explore questions thoroughly and develop evidence-informed understanding.

### 5. Graphics.

Graphics is the visual creativity and design environment. It covers graphic design, illustration, digital artwork, image creation, editing and visual communication.

**Purpose:** Help people turn ideas into useful and expressive visual work.

### 6. Music.

Music covers musical creativity and sound production, including composition, recording, arranging, editing, mixing, audio tools and music projects.

**Purpose:** Support the creation, exploration and sharing of music and sound.

### 7. Video.

Video is concerned with moving images and visual storytelling. It includes video creation, recording, editing, production techniques and the tools used to make informative or entertaining visual content.

**Purpose:** Help people create and communicate through moving images.

### 8. Broadcast.

Broadcast covers communication with an audience through radio, streaming, podcasts, live presentation, videocasts and related publishing or distribution tools.

**Purpose:** Help people present, distribute and share information and creative work.

### 9. AI Helpers.

AI Helpers explores practical uses of artificial intelligence, including AI services, assistants, prompts, workflows, reviews and responsible ways of working with AI.

**Purpose:** Make artificial intelligence useful while keeping human judgement and responsibility central.

### 10. Developer.

Developer is the technical workshop for building, maintaining and understanding digital systems. It includes Linux, operating systems, scripting, programming, automation, web development, GitHub and PlebMachine development.

**Purpose:** Build, maintain and understand useful technology.

### 11. Accounting.

Accounting covers practical financial organisation, budgeting, bookkeeping, record keeping, calculations, spreadsheets and accounting applications such as GNUCash.

**Purpose:** Make financial information clearer, more organised and more useful for decision-making.

### 12. Leisure.

Leisure is for hobbies, recreation, games, entertainment and interests pursued for enjoyment, relaxation and personal enrichment.

**Purpose:** Make room for the activities and interests that make free time rewarding.

## 🔄 How the Three Environments Work Together.

The three environments share the twelve categories, but they do not duplicate one another's functions.

PlebWare documents and publishes knowledge. PlebMachine provides practical tools and workflows through which people can apply that knowledge. Pleb Circle provides the human space where people can discuss, question, exchange experiences and help one another.

For example, a Linux subject may have explanatory articles and guides in PlebWare, practical tools and workflows in PlebMachine, and a discussion space in Pleb Circle.

A writing subject may have published stories and writing guidance in PlebWare, suitable writing tools in PlebMachine, and a Writers discussion in Pleb Circle.

The subject remains recognisable across all three environments, while each environment contributes something different.

## 🧩 Category Structure and Subgroups.

The twelve principal categories are the stable top-level structure. Subcategories and specialised discussion groups may exist beneath them when there is a clear practical reason.

A subcategory should be introduced when it gives a subject a useful identity, has enough material or activity to justify its own space, and makes the overall structure easier to navigate.

Do not create additional groups merely to increase the number of groups. Avoid duplication, overlapping purposes and unnecessary fragmentation.

For Pleb Circle, the twelve categories provide the initial group structure. Additional spaces, such as Writers and Devotions under Author, may be introduced when they support meaningful participation.

The WhatsApp community should remain a place for conversation. It should not become a duplicate of the website's full article and subcategory directory.

## 🤝 Pleb Circle — Participation and Community Care.

Pleb Circle exists for people, not simply for membership numbers.

Members are expected to introduce themselves, explain who they are and where they are from, and participate constructively. Members should respect one another, keep discussions relevant to the purpose of each group, and help maintain a welcoming and safe community.

Administrators may contact members who have not introduced themselves or whose participation is unclear. Where a membership presents a security or community-safety concern, administrators may review it and take appropriate action in accordance with the community rules.

The aim is not to intimidate newcomers. It is to establish a community in which people know who they are communicating with and can participate with confidence.

## 🛠️ Maintaining One Consistent Ecosystem.

When a category is added, renamed or substantially redefined, its purpose should be reviewed across all three environments.

PlebWare remains the published knowledge reference. PlebMachine should reflect the relevant practical tools and workflows where applicable. Pleb Circle should provide an appropriate conversation space where there is a genuine need for one.

Not every category requires identical features in every environment. Consistency means shared names, purpose and identity—not forcing every platform to behave in exactly the same way.

Changes should be documented, checked against the live repository and introduced without unnecessary disruption to existing work or community conversations.

## 🌍 The PlebWare Commitment.

PlebWare, PlebMachine and Pleb Circle are three environments serving one shared purpose: making knowledge, technology and human collaboration more accessible and useful.

The structure is designed to remain understandable as the project grows. It should reduce duplication, simplify maintenance, make navigation clearer and help people find the right place to learn, work or participate.

**Technology Must Serve Humanity.**
