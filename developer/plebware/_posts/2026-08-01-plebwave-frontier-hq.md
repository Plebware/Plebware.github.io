# Community Architecture

I have decided to consolidate my separate WhatsApp communities into a single umbrella community called **PlebWave Frontier HQ**.

Rather than having unrelated groups, I want the community to function as a coherent ecosystem built around three permanent hubs. These hubs represent the three pillars of everything I create.

## ⚒️ The Forge

**Purpose:** Build the physical world.

Topics include:

* Hardware
* Electronics
* Makerspace
* DIY
* Networking
* Robotics
* Repairs
* 3D Printing
* Engineering

The Forge represents the creation of tools and physical technology.

---

## 📜 The Scriptorium

**Purpose:** Create and preserve knowledge.

Topics include:

* Linux
* Software
* Programming
* Artificial Intelligence
* GitHub
* Documentation
* Writing
* Publishing
* Research

The Scriptorium represents ideas, software, documentation, and creative works.

---

## 🕊️ The Sanctuary

**Purpose:** Build people and community.

Topics include:

* Christian faith
* Devotionals
* Prayer
* Community discussions
* WhatsApp
* Facebook
* Instagram
* X
* YouTube
* Discord
* Telegram
* Podcasts
* Livestreams
* Community announcements

The Sanctuary is not only about faith; it is also the communication hub where content reaches people through social media and community interaction.

---

## Philosophy

The three hubs work together as one ecosystem:

* ⚒️ **The Forge** builds the tools.
* 📜 **The Scriptorium** creates the knowledge and content.
* 🕊️ **The Sanctuary** connects with and serves the people.

This reflects my belief that technology exists to serve people, knowledge empowers people, and community gives both their purpose.

I would like feedback on this architecture, suggestions for refinement, and any ideas that could make the branding stronger while keeping these three symbolic names.
