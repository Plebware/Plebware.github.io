# ⌨️ The Day the Keyboard Took Command

![Cartoon about a keyboard unexpectedly taking control of the computer.](/assets/images/keyboard-coup.webp)

The computer was behaving itself. The human was thinking. The conversation was progressing nicely.

Then something touched the keyboard.

A key was pressed. A window changed. The computer did something nobody had requested, and suddenly the session had become an investigation.

“Who authorised that?” demanded the human.

The keyboard offered no explanation. Keyboards are notoriously poor witnesses.

The incident demonstrated a simple truth about computers: they are very good at obeying input, but they do not always know whether the input was intentional. A stray key can interrupt a train of thought, trigger an action, or turn a perfectly ordinary morning into a technical mystery.

The sensible response is to check what happened, recover the work where possible, and make the setup less vulnerable to another surprise. The less sensible response is to accuse the keyboard of organising a coup—which, naturally, is much more entertaining.

The keyboard remained at its post, looking innocent.

The human returned to work, keeping a closer eye on the command centre.

**Moral of the story:** Before blaming the computer, check what it was told to do. And keep an eye on any suspicious paws or elbows near the keys.
