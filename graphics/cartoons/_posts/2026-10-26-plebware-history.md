# 📚 Every Big Project Starts Somewhere

![Cartoon representing the history and development of PlebWare.](/assets/images/plebware-history.webp)

Every project has a beginning. It rarely has a complete map.

At first, there is an idea and a person who cannot quite leave it alone. A note becomes an article. An article raises a question. The question inspires an experiment, and the experiment reveals three more things worth exploring.

Before long, the original idea has grown roots.

That is how a creative workshop can develop into something larger: a collection of writing, experiments, Linux projects, graphics, practical guides, and lessons learned along the way. Not every experiment succeeds. Some are rebuilt, some are renamed, and some become useful warnings for the next person.

A history like this is not merely a list of dates. It is a record of decisions, detours, discoveries, and persistence. It shows how a small idea can gather momentum when someone keeps returning to it and improving the work.

The best part is that the story does not end when the history is written. New tools arrive, old problems find new solutions, and another idea appears just when the workshop looks tidy.

Which, of course, means the workshop never stays tidy for long.

**Moral of the story:** A project’s history is built one decision, one lesson, and one new idea at a time.
