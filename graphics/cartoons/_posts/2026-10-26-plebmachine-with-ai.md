# 🤖 A Human, a Machine, and an AI With Ideas

![Cartoon about PlebMachine and collaboration with artificial intelligence.](/assets/images/plebmachine-with-ai.webp)

The idea sounded straightforward: build a desktop that helps people work without making decisions on their behalf.

Then the human invited AI into the workshop.

Almost immediately, ideas began multiplying. One suggestion led to another, a small improvement became a new feature, and a simple discussion threatened to become a project with its own project.

The human had to remember an important rule: the computer does not make the decision. It helps you think through the decision.

That is where PlebMachine finds its purpose. The person remains in charge, the software provides structure, and AI can help explore possibilities, explain problems, and turn an idea into a practical next step.

Of course, the AI occasionally suggests something that sounds brilliant until somebody tries it. Then the workshop gets to do what workshops do best: test, repair, learn, and try again.

By the end of the session, the machine was not magically finished. But the human had a clearer plan, a few new ideas, and a list of things to test.

That counts as progress.

**Moral of the story:** Let AI help with the thinking, but keep the human hand on the controls.
