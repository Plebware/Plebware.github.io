# 🔤 The Case of the Very Important Capital Letter

![Cartoon exploring the difference between Plebware and PlebWare.](/assets/images/Plebware-or-PlebWare.webp)

A capital letter walked into the workshop and immediately demanded recognition.

“I am only one letter,” it announced, “but apparently I have a job to do.”

The lowercase version looked unimpressed. “We are practically the same word.”

“Practically,” replied the capital letter. “But names, labels, and context can matter.”

And so began the Great Plebware Naming Debate.

In ordinary writing, people may use capitals inconsistently and still understand one another. But when a name represents a project, a website, a philosophy, or an ecosystem of work, consistency helps readers recognise what they are looking at.

PlebWare is the name used for the wider project and its online knowledge layer. The important thing is not to start a war over typography. It is to communicate clearly and use the chosen name consistently where it matters.

The capital letter eventually relaxed. It had made its point, and nobody had needed to call technical support.

**Moral of the story:** A small detail can help a name stand out—but clarity matters more than arguing about a letter.
