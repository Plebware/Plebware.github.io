# 🎧 Spotify — Your Music, Your Way.

Music can set the mood for almost anything: writing, working, exercising, relaxing, or simply enjoying a quiet moment. Spotify is a music and audio streaming service that helps listeners discover music, explore playlists, and find audio to suit different tastes.

This introduction explains what Spotify offers and how to get started. Specific features and listening options can vary by country, device, and subscription plan.

<!-- PLEBVOX:END -->

<!-- PLEBVOX:START -->

## 🎵 What Is Spotify?

Spotify is an online platform for listening to music and other audio. Listeners can search for artists, albums, tracks, playlists, and podcasts, subject to the catalogue and features available in their region.

You can use Spotify to discover familiar favourites or explore artists and genres you have not tried before. Recommendations and curated playlists can also help you find music based on your interests.

Official website: [Spotify](https://www.spotify.com/).

<!-- PLEBVOX:END -->

## 🧭 Getting Started.

1. Visit the [official Spotify website](https://www.spotify.com/).
2. Review the available account and listening options for your region.
3. Sign in or create an account if you decide to use the service.
4. Search for an artist, album, track, or playlist.
5. Save favourites and create playlists to organise music for different occasions.

Read the current Spotify information before choosing a plan. Free and paid options, where available, may differ in features, advertising, downloads, and playback controls.

## 🎼 Create Playlists for Different Activities.

Playlists make it easier to gather music for a particular mood or task. You might create:

- ✍️ **Writing and concentration:** music that helps you settle into a creative session.
- 🏋️ **Exercise:** energetic tracks for training and movement.
- 🌙 **Relaxation:** calmer music for winding down.
- 🎉 **Celebration:** favourite songs for a gathering or special occasion.
- 🧭 **Discovery:** new artists and tracks you want to explore.

There is no single correct way to organise a playlist. Choose names and collections that make sense to you, and update them as your tastes change.

## 🔑 Your First Spotify Exercise.

Try this simple activity:

1. Find three artists you already enjoy.
2. Choose one new artist or genre to explore.
3. Create a playlist for a specific activity or mood.
4. Give the playlist a clear name.
5. Revisit it later and add or remove tracks as your tastes develop.

The aim is not to collect the largest playlist. It is to make it easier to find music you genuinely enjoy.
