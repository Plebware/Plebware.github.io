# 🔑 PlebWare Update: Two Weeks of Building Behind the Scenes.

<!-- PLEBVOX:START -->

PlebWare has had a busy couple of weeks behind the scenes. While new articles have been appearing on the site, a considerable amount of work has also gone into the structure underneath them.

The aim has been simple: make PlebWare easier to navigate, easier to expand, and more useful to ordinary people.

<!-- PLEBVOX:END -->

<!-- PLEBVOX:START -->

## 📚 Research Has Grown.

The Research section has been expanded into a proper twelve-category research library.

The new structure now includes **Christian Research, Linux Research, PlebMachine Research, Web Browser, Gemini Notebook Research, Tech Research, Digital Literacy, Well Being, Sustainability, Artificial Intelligence, Cybersecurity, and Digital Society**.

Three new research areas were created: **Artificial Intelligence, Cybersecurity, and Digital Society**. The former **Notebook LM** category has also been renamed **Gemini Notebook Research** while keeping its existing folder structure so existing NotebookLM material remains organised.

The new **Digital Literacy** section has already received its first article: *The Real Cost of a “Free” App*, looking at the less obvious costs that can accompany supposedly free applications.

<!-- PLEBVOX:END -->

<!-- PLEBVOX:START -->

## 🧭 Navigation Has Been Reworked.

The site's main navigation has been rebuilt to make the twelve PlebWare modes easier to read and use across desktop, laptop, tablet and phone screens. The primary modes now use the available page width more effectively, while smaller screens use a simple two-column layout rather than squeezing the categories into narrow boxes.

The secondary navigation has also been simplified. Instead of permanently taking up space on every page, each section now has its own full-width **Sections** button. Selecting it opens that section's categories underneath, with the button changing to **Close Sections** while the menu is open. The same approach is used consistently across the PlebWare modes.

The PlebWare theme remains the central source for the site's navigation and presentation, allowing these changes to be applied consistently rather than being maintained separately on individual pages.

<!-- PLEBVOX:END -->

<!-- PLEBVOX:START -->

## 🔊 PlebVox Highlighting Has Been Improved.

The PlebVox text-to-speech system has also received structural attention. The previous visual highlighting could follow individual words, which created unnecessary visual activity and could lag behind the spoken voice on some devices.

The highlighting system is now being moved to **paragraph-level highlighting**. Instead of constantly changing the highlighted word, PlebVox can highlight the paragraph currently being read. This reduces visual churn while making it easier for a reader to follow the spoken passage.

The underlying speech engine remains responsible for the reading itself, while the new paragraph highlighting layer provides a simpler visual guide. The result is intended to be lighter, clearer and better suited to both desktop and mobile use.

<!-- PLEBVOX:END -->

<!-- PLEBVOX:START -->

## 🎓 Pleb-Tuition Has Expanded.

The Pleb-Tuition text-to-speech series has grown into a complete nine-lesson sequence, covering what text-to-speech is, Android, Samsung Galaxy, iPhone and iPad, choosing voices, making a phone read text, TalkBack and VoiceOver, troubleshooting, and a practical five-minute test passage using South African place names.

The goal is practical learning: ordinary users should be able to understand and test the technology rather than simply being told that it exists.

<!-- PLEBVOX:END -->

<!-- PLEBVOX:START -->

## 📰 The Site's Content Structure Keeps Developing.

Recent work has also included new and updated Author material, including the **Conference Table** experiment and continuing Captain Gemini news coverage. The site continues to separate everyday practical material, Author writing, Study material, Research, Developer work, and the other PlebWare modes while keeping them under one shared navigation system.

The site catalogue and recent-article system have also been kept under active attention so newly published material appears in the appropriate places.

<!-- PLEBVOX:END -->

<!-- PLEBVOX:START -->

## ⚙️ PlebMachine Development Continues Alongside PlebWare.

PlebMachine development has continued in parallel with the website, including work around the XFCE desktop, Cognitive Mode, automated modes and workspaces, dependency handling, mobile development, and the relationship between the PlebMachine desktop and the wider PlebWare ecosystem.

PlebWare is therefore not simply a collection of web pages. The website, documentation, research library, learning material, development work and supporting tools are increasingly being treated as parts of one ecosystem.

<!-- PLEBVOX:END -->

<!-- PLEBVOX:START -->

## 🔧 Why These Changes Matter.

A lot of this work is invisible when a visitor simply opens an article. That is intentional.

Good infrastructure should make the site feel simpler for the person using it.

Categories should make things easier to find. Navigation should make the site easier to understand. Learning material should build from one lesson to the next. Development work should have somewhere logical to live.

That is what the last couple of weeks have been about: **building the workshop while continuing to publish from it.**

<!-- PLEBVOX:END -->

**PlebWare keeps evolving — and the workshop remains open.**
