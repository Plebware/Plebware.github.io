# A Message From The Developer.

It has been a hectic few months building this site. 
Although I have built many websites over the last 27 years- too many to remember, but well over a hundred.
PlebWare is the first website I am truly excited about, and I can say I am proud to claim it as the best site I have built to date.

## It is really an awesome piece of machinery. 

Firstly, it is a website that makes life easier for all parties involved.
For the publishers, it allows for easy updates, with minimal knowledge of Markdown code, and even accommodates voice dictation.
For the end-user, it covers most computer-related topics and is slowly shaping up to become the envisioned learning centre it is designed to be.

## Accessibility to folks with eyesight issues.

The most important dev is that of the integrated read-aloud feature, making it easier for many folks, especially people like Juelz, my partner, who have limited eyesight.
This feature, however, is also extremely useful for busy people, as it allows for multitasking while enjoying the site content

## May to October 2026.

Over the past three to six months, from approximately May to October 2026, we have transformed PlebWare.github.io from a developing website 
into a substantial online publishing, learning, research and creative technology hub. 
We have expanded its content across Everyday, Study and Pleb-Tuition, Developer, Author, Devotions, AI Helpers, and other subject areas, 
covering everything from Linux, software, writing and graphics to practical life, health, cooking, budgeting and Christian ministry. 
We have also developed the site's identity and philosophy through the PlebWare Manifesto and articles explaining how PlebWare, PlebMachine and PlebVox fit together. 
The guiding principle throughout has been that technology should serve ordinary people, empower human creativity and make knowledge accessible rather than replace human judgement. 
By September and October, the website had grown to hundreds of published articles, providing a substantial knowledge library for visitors and collaborators.

## Connected With PlebMachine.

A second major achievement has been documenting and connecting the wider PlebMachine ecosystem. 
We have created and improved guides, technical papers, software catalogues, architecture plans and development reports, 
explaining how PlebMachine is evolving into a context-aware desktop environment for Linux, with different working modes and user experience levels. 

The website is increasingly the online knowledge layer supporting that software, 
while Pleb-Tuition is being developed to help ordinary users learn computing and technology through practical, understandable tutorials. 
We have also documented our use of AI as a collaborative development tool, alongside our human-led process of research, code review, testing, correction and approval. 
PlebVox has been integrated into the publishing experience to make suitable articles available through read-aloud functionality, 
while we have removed unreliable text-highlighting behaviour to improve cross-platform compatibility. 
The result is a closer relationship between software development, education, documentation and the public website.

## Polishing and Improving.

The third major area has been improving the website's publishing standards, navigation, discoverability and day-to-day maintenance. 
We have established clearer conventions for Jekyll front matter, distinct metadata and visible article titles, structured Markdown.
PlebVox START and END markers, and consistent copy-and-paste code. 

We have developed housekeeping tutorials and reusable code references, expanded automated article listings and the 12-mode publishing dashboard.
Recently, we started working on search-engine optimisation and structured data so that search engines can better understand the site. 
Most recently, our October work has focused on refining the SEO checks and the Personal Copy-and-Paste Codes reference.
Prioritising the snippets collaborators need most and preparing to add a Human Verified Image code. 

We have also been testing links and search-engine indexing rather than simply assuming that a successful GitHub commit means everything works. 
The overarching achievement is that PlebWare is becoming more than a collection of articles: 
It is a growing, organised and increasingly standardised publishing and learning platform, built through continuous writing, testing, correction and human oversight.
