# The PlebWare Lexicon.

## A Vocabulary for the PlebWare Ecosystem.

Words matter.

Within PlebWare, certain names have specific meanings. They describe people, ideas, projects, technologies and relationships within the wider ecosystem.

This lexicon exists to keep those meanings clear.

PlebWare is deliberately built around ordinary people using technology, creating knowledge and making things for themselves and others.

<!-- PLEBVOX:END -->

<!-- PLEBVOX:START -->

## Plebware.

**Plebware**, with a capital **P** and a lowercase **w**, refers to the **end user — the pleb, the ordinary human being behind the terminal**.

The word represents the person who uses, learns from, creates with, repairs, modifies and participates in technology.

Plebware is not defined by technical expertise, wealth, profession or status.

The fundamental idea is simple:

**The person comes first.**

<!-- PLEBVOX:END -->

<!-- PLEBVOX:START -->

## PlebWare.

**PlebWare**, with a capital **P** and capital **W**, is the **concept, ecosystem and brand**.

It encompasses the knowledge, software, writing, education, media, research, creative work, community and projects developed around the PlebWare philosophy.

The capitalization is deliberate.

**Plebware means the user.**

**PlebWare means the ecosystem.**

The name also commemorates **Plebware.com**, the original Plebware website from which the concept evolved.

<!-- PLEBVOX:END -->

<!-- PLEBVOX:START -->

## PlebMachine.

**PlebMachine** is the Linux productivity and desktop-orchestration project within the PlebWare ecosystem.

It is designed around the principle that a desktop environment should adapt to the user's needs rather than forcing the user to adapt to a fixed workflow.

PlebMachine provides a state-driven productivity layer while keeping system logic separate from user configuration.

<!-- PLEBVOX:END -->

<!-- PLEBVOX:START -->

## Plebstick.

**Plebstick**, with a capital **P** and capital **S**, is a portable flash drive containing PlebWare tools, source code, written material, documentation and other useful resources.

A Plebstick is therefore a portable piece of the PlebWare ecosystem — a practical toolbox that can travel with the Pleb.

<!-- PLEBVOX:END -->

<!-- PLEBVOX:START -->

## PlebTuition.

**PlebTuition**, with a capital **P** and capital **T**, is the educational side of the PlebWare ecosystem.

It represents tutorials, instruction, practical learning, explanations and knowledge sharing intended to help ordinary people understand and use technology.

The goal is not to make technology sound complicated.

The goal is to make complicated technology understandable.

<!-- PLEBVOX:END -->

<!-- PLEBVOX:START -->

## PlebVox.

**PlebVox** is the voice and accessibility layer used by PlebWare publications.

The name combines **Pleb** with *vox*, the Latin word for **voice**.

PlebVox provides lightweight, opt-in text-to-speech functionality so selected sections of PlebWare articles can be heard as well as read.

Its guiding principle is simple:

**Keep the article. Mark the useful sections. Let PlebVox give those sections a voice.**

<!-- PLEBVOX:END -->

<!-- PLEBVOX:START -->

## PlebTheme.

**PlebTheme** is the visual and structural theme used to present PlebWare material on the web.

It provides a common presentation layer through which PlebWare articles, documentation, projects and other resources can be organised.

<!-- PLEBVOX:END -->

<!-- PLEBVOX:START -->

## PlebWave.

**PlebWave** refers to the communication and broadcasting side of the ecosystem.

It represents the movement of PlebWare ideas, information, media and creative work outward toward other people.

PlebWave may include written, spoken, visual and multimedia forms of communication.

<!-- PLEBVOX:END -->

<!-- PLEBVOX:START -->

## PlebWave Frontier.

**PlebWave Frontier** is part of the wider people and community side of the PlebWare ecosystem.

It represents a place where ideas, creativity, communication and collaboration can meet.

The Frontier is where the next experiment begins.

<!-- PLEBVOX:END -->

<!-- PLEBVOX:START -->

## The Circle.

**The Circle** is a community-oriented part of the wider PlebWare ecosystem.

It provides a place for people to gather around shared interests, communication, creativity and collaboration.

The Circle is about the people participating in the ecosystem rather than the technology itself.

<!-- PLEBVOX:END -->

## The Pleb.

The **Pleb** is the ordinary person.

Not a lesser person.

Not an inferior person.

Simply the human being who ultimately has to live with, understand, operate and benefit from the technology that other people create.

The Pleb is therefore not the lowest part of the system.

**The Pleb is the reason the system exists.**

<!-- PLEBVOX:END -->

<!-- PLEBVOX:START -->

## Human Behind the Terminal.

The **Human Behind the Terminal** is one of the central ideas of PlebWare.

Behind every keyboard, touchscreen, command line, application and artificial intelligence system is a person.

PlebWare puts that person back into the centre of the conversation.

<!-- PLEBVOX:END -->

<!-- PLEBVOX:START -->

## PlebWare Philosophy.

The **PlebWare Philosophy** can be reduced to a few simple principles:

- Technology should serve people.
- Software should be understandable.
- Knowledge should be shareable.
- Open-source tools should remain accessible.
- Artificial intelligence should assist rather than dominate.
- The desktop should adapt to the user's needs.
- Documentation should be written for humans.
- Accessibility should be considered from the beginning.
- Creativity belongs to everyone.
- The human remains the final authority over the machine.

<!-- PLEBVOX:END -->

## The PlebWare Family.

The PlebWare ecosystem is not one application.

It is a collection of ideas and projects built around the same underlying concept.

**Plebware** — the human behind the terminal.

**PlebWare** — the ecosystem and brand.

**PlebMachine** — the Linux productivity layer.

**Plebstick** — the portable PlebWare toolbox.

**PlebTuition** — the educational side of the ecosystem.

**PlebVox** — the voice and accessibility layer.

**PlebTheme** — the presentation layer.

**PlebWave Frontier** — the communications and community frontier.

And the **PlebWare Lexicon** — the dictionary that keeps the language together.

## Why "Pleb"?

The word *pleb* has historically been used dismissively.

PlebWare turns that idea on its head.

The ordinary person does not need to be a programmer, engineer, scientist or technology professional to deserve useful technology.

The Pleb is the person technology is supposed to help.

So PlebWare takes the word back.

**Pleb does not mean stupid.**

**Pleb means human.**
