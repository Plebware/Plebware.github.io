# 🔑 The PlebWare Lexicon

## A Vocabulary for the PlebWare Ecosystem

Words matter.

Within PlebWare, certain names have specific meanings. They describe people, ideas, projects, technologies and relationships within the wider ecosystem.

This lexicon exists to keep those meanings clear.

PlebWare is deliberately built around ordinary people using technology, creating knowledge and making things for themselves and others.

<!-- PLEBVOX:END -->

## 🔑 Plebware

<!-- PLEBVOX:START -->

**Plebware** refers to the **user — the ordinary human being**.

The word represents the person who uses, learns from, creates with, repairs, modifies and participates in technology.

Plebware is not defined by technical expertise, wealth, profession or status.

A Plebware user does not have to be a programmer, engineer or specialist.

The fundamental idea is simple:

**The person comes first.**

<!-- PLEBVOX:END -->

---

## 🔑 PlebWare

<!-- PLEBVOX:START -->

**PlebWare** is the **concept, ecosystem and brand**.

It encompasses the knowledge, software, writing, education, media, research, creative work, community and projects developed around the PlebWare philosophy.

The capitalization is deliberate.

**Plebware** means the user.

**PlebWare** means the ecosystem.

The two concepts are connected:

**Plebware creates, uses and participates in PlebWare.**

<!-- PLEBVOX:END -->

## 🧠 PlebWare Ecosystem

<!-- PLEBVOX:START -->

The **PlebWare Ecosystem** is the complete collection of people, knowledge, software, projects, media, education, community and ideas connected to PlebWare.

It is not a single application.

## 📚 Knowledge Modes

<!-- PLEBVOX:START -->

PlebWare organizes its knowledge into **twelve Knowledge Modes**.

These provide broad areas for discovering and organizing information:

**Everyday, Author, Study, Research, Graphics, Music, Video, Broadcast, AI Helpers, Developer, Accounting and Leisure.**

The Knowledge Modes are not intended to restrict what an article can discuss.

They provide a practical way of navigating a large and growing knowledge library.

<!-- PLEBVOX:END -->

---

<!-- PLEBVOX:START -->

## 🐧 PlebMachine

<!-- PLEBVOX:START -->

**PlebMachine** is a modular productivity layer for Linux.

It is designed around the principle that a desktop environment should remain understandable, configurable and useful to the person operating it.

PlebMachine separates system logic from user configuration and aims to provide a state-driven approach to desktop orchestration rather than imposing a fixed desktop structure.

<!-- PLEBVOX:END -->

---

## 🎧 PlebVox

<!-- PLEBVOX:START -->

**PlebVox** comes from the Latin word *vox*, meaning **voice**.

PlebVox is an optional, lightweight text-to-speech system for PlebWare articles.

Authors can place PlebVox markers around selected sections of an article. Those sections can then be read aloud using the browser's built-in speech synthesis capabilities.

PlebVox does not require an article to be rewritten and does not automatically read every article.

Its guiding principle is:

**Keep the article. Mark the useful sections. Let PlebVox give those sections a voice.**

<!-- PLEBVOX:END -->

## 📡 PlebWave

<!-- PLEBVOX:START -->

**PlebWave** refers to the broadcasting and communication side of the ecosystem.

It represents the movement of PlebWare ideas, information, media and creative work outward toward other people.

PlebWave may include written, spoken, visual and multimedia forms of communication.

<!-- PLEBVOX:END -->

---

## 🌊 PlebWave Frontier

<!-- PLEBVOX:START -->

**PlebWave Frontier** is part of the wider people and community side of the PlebWare ecosystem.

It represents a place where ideas, creativity, communication and collaboration can meet.

PlebWave Frontier is therefore better understood as a community and communication concept than simply as a software project.

<!-- PLEBVOX:END -->

---

## 👥 The Circle

<!-- PLEBVOX:START -->

**The Circle** is a community-oriented part of the wider PlebWare ecosystem.

It provides a place for people to gather around shared interests, communication, creativity and collaboration.

The Circle is about the people participating in the ecosystem rather than the technology itself.

<!-- PLEBVOX:END -->

---

## 🛠️ Projects

<!-- PLEBVOX:START -->

A **PlebWare Project** is a specific piece of work developed within the wider ecosystem.

Projects may involve software, hardware, writing, graphics, media, research, education or other forms of creation.

A project does not have to become a separate company or product.

It can simply be an experiment, tool, idea or working system that contributes something useful to the ecosystem.

<!-- PLEBVOX:END -->

---

## 🔗 Repositories

<!-- PLEBVOX:START -->

A **repository** is a technical container used to develop, store and publish code, documentation or other project material through GitHub.

A GitHub repository is not automatically the same thing as a PlebWare concept.

Repositories are implementation structures.

PlebWare concepts describe the human and functional relationships between those implementations.

<!-- PLEBVOX:END -->

---

## 🧩 PlebCore

<!-- PLEBVOX:START -->

**PlebCore** refers to the underlying technology and core systems that support parts of the PlebWare ecosystem.

The term may be used for foundational software, technical infrastructure or core components developed within the wider PlebWare environment.

Its exact scope may evolve as the ecosystem develops.

<!-- PLEBVOX:END -->

---

## 🔑 The PlebWare Philosophy

<!-- PLEBVOX:START -->

The PlebWare philosophy can be expressed simply:

**People first.**

Technology should serve people.

Knowledge should be understandable.

Software should be adaptable.

Creative tools should be accessible.

Systems should not deliberately hide their workings behind unnecessary complexity.

And ordinary people should be able to learn, create and participate.

PlebWare is an attempt to put those principles into practice.

<!-- PLEBVOX:END -->

---

## 📖 A Living Lexicon

<!-- PLEBVOX:START -->

This lexicon is not intended to be frozen.

As PlebWare develops, new projects, technologies and concepts may require new terminology.

Existing definitions may also be refined when their meaning becomes clearer.

The purpose of the Lexicon is therefore not merely to define words.

It is to preserve the **relationships and ideas behind the words**.

<!-- PLEBVOX:END -->

            const newTheme = getTheme();

        const observer = new MutationObserver(function(mutations) {
            mutations.forEach(function(mutation) {
                if (mutation.attributeName === 'class') {
                    onThemeChange();
                }
            });
        });

        if (document.readyState === 'loading') {

                init();

                observer.observe(document.body, {
