# 🖥️🤖 Local Artificial Intelligence

Local Artificial Intelligence is the practice of running Artificial Intelligence models directly on your own computer instead of relying only on online services.

It represents a growing movement toward personal computing, privacy, independence, and open-source innovation.

With Local Artificial Intelligence, users can explore, create, research, write, code, and experiment with Artificial Intelligence while keeping more control over their own data.

<!-- PLEBVOX:END -->

<!-- PLEBVOX:START -->

## 🔑 What Is Local Artificial Intelligence?

Local Artificial Intelligence means Artificial Intelligence systems that operate on your personal device.

Instead of sending every request to a remote server, Local Artificial Intelligence allows users to run models using their own:

* Desktop computers
* Laptops
* Workstations
* Home servers

Local Artificial Intelligence can assist with:

* Writing
* Coding
* Research
* Image generation
* Document analysis
* Learning
* Automation

<!-- PLEBVOX:END -->

## 🔑 Why Use Local Artificial Intelligence?

Online Artificial Intelligence services are powerful, but Local Artificial Intelligence offers different advantages.

Benefits may include:

* Greater privacy
* Offline access
* Control over data
* Reduced dependence on online services
* Experimentation with open-source models
* Personal Artificial Intelligence environments

Local Artificial Intelligence brings Artificial Intelligence closer to the user.

## 🔑 How Local Artificial Intelligence Works

A Local Artificial Intelligence system usually consists of:

* An Artificial Intelligence model
* Software to run the model
* Hardware resources
* User instructions

The computer processes requests locally rather than sending everything elsewhere.

Performance depends on:

* Processor power
* Memory
* Storage
* Graphics capability
* Model size
