# 🤖 Getting Started with AI Helpers PlebMachine User (Mode 09).

Welcome to **AI Helpers PlebMachine User**, the PlebWare learning section connected to PlebMachine Mode 09.

AI Helpers mode helps users choose and work with Artificial Intelligence tools deliberately. It treats Artificial Intelligence as assistance for thinking and doing—not as an authority that replaces human judgement, responsibility, or control.

## What This Mode Is For.

This mode is intended for people who want to use their computer for brainstorming, drafting, summarising, coding assistance, learning support, comparing Artificial Intelligence tools, and improving workflows. It offers a recognisable starting point for relevant guidance without suggesting that every user must work in exactly the same way.

## How PlebMachine and PlebWare Work Together.

PlebMachine helps organise the computing environment around the user's chosen activity. PlebWare provides the knowledge layer: explanations, tutorials, recommended applications, reference material, and practical examples that help users learn and work with confidence.

The computer does not make the decision. It helps you think through the decision. You remain free to use your desktop, applications, and preferred workflow.

## What You Will Find Here.

As this section develops, it will gather:

- **Tutorials and how-to guides** for common tasks and new skills.
- **Tools and application guidance** relevant to this mode.
- **Reference resources** that make useful information easier to find.
- **Practical workflows** for solving problems and completing projects.
- **Tips for working safely and effectively**, including checking information and keeping suitable backups.

## Suggested Starting Points.

Topics that may be useful in this mode include writing effective prompts, checking outputs, protecting private information, understanding limitations, and keeping humans responsible for decisions. Recommended applications may include Artificial Intelligence services and local or desktop helpers selected for the task, privacy needs, and available hardware. These are starting points, not requirements; choose tools that suit your needs, abilities, hardware, and preferences.

## Keep Learning at Your Own Pace.

You do not need to master everything at once. Start with the task in front of you, use the relevant tutorial or resource, and build on what you learn. New articles will appear in this section as PlebWare's learning library grows.
